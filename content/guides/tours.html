How to choose a Shogran tour

If you only have one day

The 1-day tour leaves Islamabad or Rawalpindi before dawn, because Shogran is about 250 km and 5-6 hours away. It works for students and working professionals, but it is a long day. If you can spare a night, the 2-day options are far more relaxed and give you a better chance of a clear morning at Siri Paye.

If Siri Paye is your main goal

Siri Paye sits about 8-10 km above Shogran at roughly 9,500 ft. The only practical way up is a 4×4 jeep on a rough track, about 45-60 minutes each way. Clouds often settle over the meadow by late morning, so tours that include a night in Shogran let you leave at 7 AM and see the lakes and Makra Peak at their best.

If you love forests and quiet

Sharan Forest above Paras is one of the densest pine, fir and deodar forests in the Kaghan Valley, and it is much quieter than Siri Paye. Pick a Shogran and Sharan tour, or add the Manna Meadows Explorer if you are already staying in Shogran.

If you are travelling as a family

The Family Tour keeps driving days short, uses AC transport and stays in hotels with heating and hot water. Families with small children usually prefer two nights in one hotel rather than moving every day.

If you want snow

Shogran gets regular snowfall from December to February. The Winter Snow Tour runs only when the road from Kiwai is open, and the Siri Paye track is closed in winter. We watch road conditions daily and will tell you honestly if a date is risky.

What is usually included

  • Transport from Islamabad, Rawalpindi or Lahore in an AC vehicle, with a driver who knows the Kaghan road
  • Hotel nights in Shogran, Malkandi, Paras or Naran, depending on the tour
  • 4×4 jeeps for Siri Paye and Sharan Forest, where the route needs them
  • Breakfasts and dinners on multi-day tours, as listed on each tour page
  • A tour guide, and on group tours DSLR photography and drone coverage

Lunches, personal shopping, horse rides and entry tickets are usually extra. Every tour page lists its own inclusions, and your confirmation message spells them out again.

How many days do you need for Shogran?

Two nights is ideal. It lets you drive up from Islamabad on day one, visit Siri Paye early on day two and travel back on day three. A day trip is possible but very long, since Shogran is 5-6 hours from Islamabad each way.

Do Shogran tours include the Siri Paye jeep?

Most of our multi-day tours include the 4×4 jeep to Siri Paye. Each tour page lists exactly which jeeps are included, and you can add a Siri Paye jeep to any hotel booking.

Can I join a group tour as a single traveller or couple?

Yes. Group tours run on fixed dates and charge per person, so couples and solo travellers can join. Some tours need a minimum of two or four people to run.

Do you run tours from Lahore?

Yes. Our 3-day Shogran and Sharan tours and the Naran Kaghan tour depart from Lahore, with Islamabad pickup on the way. Lahore to Shogran is about 600 km, which is why Lahore tours are at least three days.
