Why the BR-V suits the Kaghan Valley

Families heading to Shogran usually face a choice between a comfortable sedan that is too small and a 4×4 that is too expensive. The BR-V sits neatly between them. Its seven seats fit grandparents, parents and children in one vehicle, and the automatic gearbox makes the hairpin climb from Kiwai to Shogran smooth for everyone on board.

Ground clearance matters more than people expect. The road between Balakot and Kiwai has rough and broken patches, especially after the monsoon, and the BR-V's higher stance rides over them where a sedan has to slow right down. Dual AC keeps the back rows cool on a hot June afternoon around Mansehra, and the roof rails give extra space for luggage on longer trips.

When the BR-V is not enough

The BR-V is not a 4×4. From December to February snow and ice on the climb from Kiwai call for a Prado or Fortuner, and for Siri Paye or Sharan Forest you still need local jeeps.
