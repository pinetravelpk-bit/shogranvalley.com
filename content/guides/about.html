Facts we keep consistent everywhere

Travel information about Shogran varies a lot across the internet. These are the figures we use on every page, based on our own trips and cross-checked with public sources:

  • Shogran altitude: 7,749 ft (2,362 m), coordinates about 34.633 N, 73.467 E
  • Islamabad to Shogran: about 250 km, usually 5-6 hours
  • Kiwai to Shogran: a steep 7-9 km climb off the Kaghan road
  • Siri Paye: 8-10 km above Shogran at roughly 9,500 ft, 45-60 minutes by 4×4 jeep
  • Shogran to Naran: about 60 km, 2-2½ hours
  • Best time to visit: May to September; snow season: December to February
  • No ATM in Shogran; the last reliable ATM is in Balakot
