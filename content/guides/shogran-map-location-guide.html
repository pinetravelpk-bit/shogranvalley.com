Offline map checklist for the Kaghan Valley

Quick answer: Before leaving Islamabad, download an offline map covering Mansehra, Balakot, Kiwai, Shogran, Paras and Naran, save your hotel as a pin, and note the key landmarks: Balakot (last reliable ATMs), Kiwai (turn for Shogran and Kawai Waterfall), Shogran at about 34.633 N, 73.467 E, and Siri Paye about 8-10 km above it.

  1. Download the area offline in your maps app while you still have good signal in Islamabad.
  2. Pin your hotel and the Shogran Valley office at Main Shogran Bazar.
  3. Save key stops: Mansehra, Balakot, Kiwai and Kawai Waterfall, Paras, Kaghan town, Naran.
  4. Screenshot the route in case your phone app struggles without data.
  5. Write down phone numbers: your hotel, your driver and our team on +92 343 5853835.

Should I download offline maps for Shogran?

Yes. Signal is patchy on parts of the Kaghan road and above Shogran, so an offline map and saved pins make navigation much easier.

What is the turn-off for Shogran?

Kiwai on the Kaghan road, about 34 km past Balakot. A steep road climbs 7-9 km from Kiwai to Shogran.
