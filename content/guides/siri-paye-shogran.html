Photography tips for Siri Paye

Quick answer: For the best Siri Paye photos, arrive by about 7:30 AM for still water and soft light, shoot the small lakes with Makra Peak reflected in them, include people or horses for scale on the wide meadow, and visit in June or September for the clearest skies.

Gear and practical notes

  • Charge batteries the night before; generator hours in Shogran can be limited.
  • Carry a lens cloth; mist and drizzle are common in monsoon months.
  • Drone users should fly considerately, away from people and animals, and respect local rules.
  • Ask before photographing shepherds and their families.

When is the best light at Siri Paye?

Early morning, roughly 7:30 to 9:30 AM, when the water is still and the light is soft, before clouds tend to build later in the morning.

Can I fly a drone at Siri Paye?

Many visitors do, but fly considerately, keep away from people and animals, and follow any local rules or instructions on the day.
