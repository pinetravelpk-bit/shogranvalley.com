Which vehicle to choose for your season

Quick answer: In dry summer weather, any healthy car such as a Suzuki Cultus or Toyota Corolla reaches Shogran. In monsoon rain (July and August), higher clearance helps, so a Honda BR-V or a 4×4 is safer. In winter snow (December to February), use a 4×4 such as the Toyota Fortuner or Prado. For Siri Paye, always switch to a local 4×4 jeep in Shogran.

If your own car struggles on the climb

If the 7-9 km climb from Kiwai looks too much for your car, especially in rain or with a heavy load, park safely at Kiwai and take a local jeep up for about PKR 3,500 per jeep. Many hotels can arrange this, and our team can book it for you on WhatsApp at +92 343 5853835.

Can I leave my car at Kiwai and take a jeep to Shogran?

Yes. A local jeep from Kiwai to Shogran costs about PKR 3,500 per jeep, a good option in wet weather or for small, heavily loaded cars.

What is the best car to rent for Shogran in summer?

A Suzuki Cultus for couples on a budget, a Toyota Corolla for comfort, or a Honda BR-V for families of up to seven, all with a driver.
