Why stay in Shogran Bazar

Shogran Bazar is the small centre of the plateau, at about 7,749 ft. Staying here puts you within walking distance of tea shops, small restaurants and the jeep stand for Siri Paye, and close to the forest walks that start around the plateau. For budget travellers it is the most convenient place to be: you can walk to dinner, meet your jeep at 7 AM without a drive, and pick up snacks before a day out.

Tips for a budget Shogran trip

  • Share a Siri Paye jeep. Jeeps are priced per vehicle, about PKR 12,000 return, so a group of six pays far less per person.
  • Travel mid-week. Rooms are easier to find and sometimes cheaper outside weekends and Eid.
  • Carry cash. There is no ATM in Shogran; withdraw in Balakot.
  • Eat local. Pakistani home-style food in the bazaar is the best value meal on the plateau.
  • Pack warm. Even budget travellers need a jacket; nights are cool in summer and freezing in winter.

Getting to Shogran Bazar

From Islamabad, follow the Hazara Motorway to Mansehra, then Balakot and the Kaghan road to Kiwai, about 240 km. From Kiwai, a steep 7-9 km road climbs to Shogran. Total time is usually 5-6 hours. If you arrive by bus in Balakot or Kiwai, a local jeep can take you up the climb; the Kiwai to Shogran jeep costs about PKR 3,500 per jeep.

Budget packing checklist for Shogran

  • A warm jacket and a fleece, even in summer; nights on the plateau are cool
  • Comfortable walking shoes for forest paths and the Siri Paye meadow
  • A small torch, as power cuts are common in the valley
  • Your CNIC or passport for hotel check-in and police check posts
  • Enough cash for rooms, food, jeeps and tips, since there is no ATM in Shogran
  • A refillable water bottle and a bag to carry your rubbish back down
