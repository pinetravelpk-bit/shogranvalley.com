Why book a Civic for the mountains?

A Shogran trip is mostly a road trip. From Islamabad, you spend around 5-6 hours on the Hazara Motorway (E-35) to Mansehra, the busy road through Garhi Habibullah and Balakot, and the Kaghan road along the Kunhar River to Kiwai, before the 7-9 km climb up to Shogran. The Civic makes those hours genuinely relaxing. The cabin is quiet, the turbocharged engine overtakes slow trucks on the Mansehra to Balakot stretch with ease, and the automatic gearbox keeps the hairpin climb from Kiwai smooth.

The sunroof is a small luxury that pays off in the valley, where tall pine forest and steep ridges rise above the road. Many couples book the Civic for honeymoons and anniversaries for exactly this reason.

Who is the Civic for?

  • Honeymoon couples pairing it with a room at Pine Park Hotel and Resorts or Grand Vista Hotel and our VIP honeymoon tour.
  • Executives and guests visiting the Kaghan Valley for work, events or retreats.
  • Overseas visitors arriving at Islamabad International Airport who want a comfortable first drive into the mountains.
  • Small families who value comfort over extra seats.

The Civic sits low, so it is a summer and dry-weather car for the main roads. It does not go up the Siri Paye track or forest tracks such as Sharan; local 4×4 jeeps do those trips.

Sample VIP weekend in the Civic

  1. Friday: Afternoon pickup in Islamabad, dinner on the way, check in at Pine Park, Shogran.
  2. Saturday: Sunrise jeep to Siri Paye, breakfast on the meadow, slow afternoon in the pine forest, bonfire evening at the hotel.
  3. Sunday: Late breakfast, Kawai Waterfall on the way down, back in Islamabad by evening.

Booking tips

  • Book at least a week ahead for June to August and Eid; premium sedans are limited.
  • Share flight details for airport pickups so we can track delays.
  • Your quote confirms what is included, such as fuel, tolls and the driver's stay.
  • Carry cash for jeeps and tips; there is no ATM in Shogran.

Can a Honda Civic drive to Shogran?

Yes, in dry weather. The climb from Kiwai is steep with tight hairpins, but the Civic's automatic gearbox and turbo engine handle it well with our experienced drivers. In snow, choose a 4×4.
