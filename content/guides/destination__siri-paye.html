Siri Paye Meadows complete guide: jeep, lakes, timing and tips

Quick answer: Siri Paye is a high alpine meadow about 8-10 km above Shogran at roughly 9,500 ft in the Kaghan Valley, Mansehra District. You reach it by 4×4 jeep from Shogran in 45-60 minutes each way, or on a 3-4 hour uphill hike. The track is usually open from about May to October. Leave Shogran by 7 AM to see the two small lakes, Siri and Paye, reflecting Makra Peak before clouds roll in.

The name joins two places: Siri, a small lake on the way up, and Paye, the high meadow near the top with its own small lake. Together they form the best-known viewpoint in the lower Kaghan Valley, with rolling pastures, pine forest below and the snow-streaked ridge of Makra Peak behind. In summer, shepherds bring their flocks up from the valley, and simple tea stalls and horse rides appear on the meadow.

Ordinary cars cannot climb the Siri Paye track. It is narrow, unpaved and rutted, with loose stones and mud after rain. Local jeeps are driven by experienced drivers who use it every day. You can book a Siri Paye 4×4 jeep with your hotel, priced per jeep rather than per person, so a family or small group shares one vehicle.

Time of day matters as much as the month. Warm air rising from the valley often brings cloud over the meadow by late morning, hiding the peak and the lakes. A 6:30 to 7 AM departure from Shogran gives you the best chance of clear reflections.

What to do at Siri Paye

  • Walk to the lakes. The two small lakes sit in shallow hollows of the meadow. On still mornings they mirror the surrounding ridges.
  • Photograph Makra Peak. The snow-streaked peak rises above the meadow and is the classic backdrop for Siri Paye photos.
  • Ride a horse. Local handlers offer short pony rides across the meadow. Agree the price before you start.
  • Have tea on the grass. In season, tea stalls serve tea, pakoras and simple snacks.
  • Hike further. Fit walkers can continue up the slopes towards the Makra Peak base, ideally with a local guide.

What to bring

  • A warm jacket, even in July; the meadow is about 1,750 ft higher than Shogran and can be windy
  • Comfortable shoes with grip for wet grass and muddy patches
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses; the sun is strong at altitude
  • Cash in small notes for tea, horse rides and tips, as there is no ATM in Shogran
  • A rubbish bag, so nothing is left on the meadow

Can you walk from Shogran to Siri Paye?

Yes. The uphill hike is about 8-10 km and takes 3-4 hours for a fit walker. Many people hike up and take a jeep down, or the other way round.

Is Siri Paye safe for children and older people?

Yes, by jeep. The ride is bumpy but the drivers are experienced. The meadow itself is gentle. Take it slowly if anyone is not used to altitude.

What is the altitude of Siri Paye?

Siri Paye is at roughly 9,500 ft (about 2,900 m), around 1,750 ft higher than Shogran at 7,749 ft (2,362 m).

Can you camp at Siri Paye?

Camping is possible in summer, but nights are cold and weather changes quickly. Arrange it through a local operator and never camp alone.
