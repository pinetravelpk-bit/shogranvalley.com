Best time to visit Shogran by type of traveller

Quick answer: Families do best in June, honeymoon couples in June or September, photographers in September and early October, budget travellers mid-week in May or September, and snow lovers between December and February. July and August are the greenest but also the busiest and wettest weeks.

Dates to avoid if you dislike crowds

  • Eid holidays, when the Kaghan road and hotels are at their busiest
  • Weekends in July and August during school holidays
  • Long public holiday weekends

If you must travel then, book rooms two to three weeks ahead and start your drive very early.

When is the best time to visit Shogran with children?

June. The weather is warm, mornings at Siri Paye are often clear, and the monsoon rains of July and August have not yet arrived.

When is Shogran best for photography?

September and early October, when the air is clear, the grass turns golden and there are far fewer visitors.
