Why the high roof matters

The high roof is not just a look. It gives standing headroom inside, so passengers can move to the back rows without crouching, and it makes loading easier at hotels and rest stops. It also creates a large luggage area above and behind the rear seats. For a group with suitcases, sleeping bags, snacks and warm clothes for the cold Shogran nights, that space is the difference between a comfortable trip and a cramped one.

On the road to Shogran

The Hiroof follows the Hazara Motorway to Mansehra, then Balakot and the Kaghan road along the Kunhar River to Kiwai, about 240 km. The diesel engine climbs the 7-9 km hairpin road to Shogran steadily with a full load in dry weather, driven slowly by experienced drivers. Expect 5-6 hours from Islamabad plus stops. For Siri Paye, a group of 14 needs two or three local Siri Paye jeeps, priced per jeep.

Sample 3-day plan for 14

  1. Day 1: Early start from Islamabad, breakfast near Mansehra, Kawai Waterfall, arrive in Shogran for lunch.
  2. Day 2: 7 AM Siri Paye jeeps, afternoon football or walks in the Shogran forest, bonfire night.
  3. Day 3: Drive to Paras for a quick river stop, then return to Islamabad.
