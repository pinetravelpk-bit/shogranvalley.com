Winter snow tour to Shogran: what to expect from December to February

Quick answer: Our winter snow tour is a 2-day, 1-night trip from Islamabad and Rawalpindi to snow-covered Shogran, run in December, January and February. It costs about PKR 15,000 per person (minimum 4) and includes 4×4 transport both ways (essential on snowy roads), one night in a Shogran hotel, a bonfire and barbecue dinner, breakfast, a local guide, sleds and tolls. Nights can drop to about -10°C, and road conditions decide the exact dates, so flexibility is essential.

Itinerary

Day 1: Islamabad to snowy Shogran

Pickup around 5 AM and drive via Abbottabad, Mansehra and Balakot, with a breakfast stop. At Kiwai the group switches to heavy-duty 4×4 jeeps for the snowy climb to Shogran. After arriving around midday, spend the afternoon in the snow: snowball fights, sledging and photos among frozen pines. In the evening, warm up by the bonfire with a barbecue dinner at the hotel.

Day 2: Snow morning and return

Wake to snow-covered mountains for sunrise photos, then a hot breakfast and more time in the snow. Check out around midday and head back down to Kiwai, arriving in Islamabad or Rawalpindi in the evening.

Snow, roads and safety

Shogran at 7,749 ft gets regular snow from December to February, sometimes into March. The 7-9 km climb from Kiwai is steep and narrow, and after heavy snowfall it can close for a day or two until it is cleared. That is why this tour uses 4×4 vehicles and why we watch conditions closely.

  • We check road status the day before and the morning of departure.
  • If the road is closed, we will suggest new dates rather than take risks.
  • The Siri Paye track above Shogran is closed in winter; the snow fun happens on the Shogran plateau.
  • Expect slower driving on ice and packed snow, so the drive can take longer than in summer.

Is a winter trip right for you?

Winter Shogran is magical but demanding. It suits groups who are happy in the cold, flexible on dates and excited by snow. Families with very young children, people sensitive to cold and travellers on a fixed one-weekend schedule may prefer to visit between May and October, when Siri Paye is also open.

Hotels in winter
