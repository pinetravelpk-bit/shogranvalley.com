Shinkiari Hut, Paras: what to know before you go

Quick answer: Shinkiari Hut is a quiet forest stop in the Paras area of the Kaghan Valley, Mansehra District, close to Sharan Forest, Malkandi and Manna Meadows. It is reached by local jeep, and access depends on the season and the state of the forest tracks, so always check conditions with a local team before you plan a visit. Not to be confused with Shinkiari town

Many people searching for "Shinkiari" find information about Shinkiari town, a separate place in Mansehra District on the Karakoram Highway north of Mansehra city. That town is on a different road and is not in the Kaghan Valley. Shinkiari Hut on this page is a forest stop in the Paras area, along the Kaghan road towards Naran. If you are planning a route, make sure your driver knows you mean the Paras area, not the town on the Karakoram Highway.

How to plan a visit

Because conditions on forest tracks around Paras change with rain, snow and maintenance, Shinkiari Hut is best treated as an add-on to a trip that is already planned around better-known places nearby, rather than as the main goal of a journey.

  1. Base yourself nearby. Stay in Shogran, Malkandi or Paras. Riverside hotels in Malkandi and Paras are the closest.
  2. Ask about the track the day before. Your hotel or our team can check whether jeeps are running and how long the trip takes.
  3. Use a local jeep and driver. Do not attempt forest tracks in an ordinary car.
  4. Combine it. Pair it with Sharan Forest or Malkandi Forest so the day is worthwhile whatever the conditions.
  5. Start early. Mountain weather often changes in the afternoon, and you want to be back on the main road before dark.

Getting to the Paras area

From Islamabad, follow the Hazara Motorway to Mansehra, then the Kaghan road via Balakot and Kiwai to Paras, roughly 255 km and 5½-6½ hours in normal traffic. From Shogran, drive 7-9 km down to Kiwai and continue up the valley to Paras. Malkandi is further along the same road.

A sample forest day from Malkandi

  1. 7:30 AM: Breakfast at your hotel in Malkandi or Paras.
  2. 8:30 AM: Meet your jeep and driver in Paras after confirming track conditions.
  3. Morning: Visit Sharan Forest and walk among the tall conifers while the air is cool.
  4. Midday: Picnic lunch, then continue to other forest stops in the Paras area, such as Shinkiari Hut, if the driver confirms the track is passable.
  5. Afternoon: Return to the main road and your hotel before the light fades.

Planning the day this way means you still enjoy a full forest outing even if one track turns out to be closed.

Safety tips for forest stops

  • Tell your hotel where you are going and when you expect to return; mobile signal is patchy in the forest.
  • Carry water, a warm layer and a rain jacket.
  • Bring cash; there is no ATM in Shogran or Paras, and the last reliable ATM is in Balakot.
  • Leave no litter and do not light fires outside designated places.
