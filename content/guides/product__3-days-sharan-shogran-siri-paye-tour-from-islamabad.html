How the three days work

Day 1: Islamabad to Shogran

Leave Islamabad at 7 AM in comfortable transport and follow the Hazara Motorway to Mansehra, then Balakot. Short stops at Balakot and Kawai (Kiwai) Waterfall break the journey before the climb to Shogran at 7,749 ft. Check in at a family hotel, explore the Shogran meadows, and enjoy evening tea with mountain views.

Day 2: Siri Paye and Sharan Forest

An early jeep ride takes you up the rough track to the Siri Paye meadows at about 9,500 ft, where small lakes and Makra Peak make the classic Kaghan Valley view. Later, travel down the valley to Paras and up by jeep into Sharan Forest for a night among the pines, with a live BBQ and a bonfire if the weather allows.

Day 3: Sharan to Islamabad

Wake up in the forest, enjoy breakfast and a short walk, then return by jeep to the main road and drive back to Islamabad, arriving in the evening.

The exact order of Siri Paye and Sharan can change with weather and track conditions; your guide confirms the plan.

Why spend a night in Sharan

Most visitors see Sharan Forest only as a day trip. Staying overnight changes the experience: the jeeps leave, the forest goes quiet, and you see the trees in the evening light and early morning mist. Accommodation at Sharan is simple, so think of it as a forest cottage or lodge experience rather than a hotel resort.

What to bring

  • Warm clothes for Siri Paye and the Sharan night
  • Walking shoes and a small daypack
  • A torch, as forest lodges can have limited lighting
  • Cash for lunches, tea and extras; there is no ATM in Shogran or Sharan
  • CNIC or passport for check-in
