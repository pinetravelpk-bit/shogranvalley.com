Manna Meadows Explorer tour from Shogran: details, timing and what to bring

Quick answer: The Manna Meadows Explorer is a full-day jeep and trek tour that starts in Shogran Bazar and visits Manna Meadows, a quiet high pasture surrounded by pine forest. It costs about PKR 8,000 per person (minimum 2) and includes a 4×4 jeep both ways, an experienced local guide, tea during the trek and an evening bonfire when weather allows. Pickup is at 8 AM from your Shogran hotel and drop-off around 6:30 PM. Meals, hotel and transport from Islamabad are not included.

How the day unfolds

  1. 8:00 AM: Pickup from your hotel in Shogran.
  2. 8:30 AM: Jeep drive along the forest track towards the Manna trail.
  3. Around 9 AM: Start the trek, or continue by jeep where the track allows.
  4. Mid-morning: Arrive at Manna Meadows for wide views and photos.
  5. Late morning to early afternoon: Explore the meadows and streams, look for birds and wildlife.
  6. Around 2 PM: Picnic lunch on the meadow (bring your own or ask us to arrange).
  7. Late afternoon: Bonfire if the weather is kind, then the return journey.
  8. Around 6:30 PM: Back at your hotel in Shogran.

Why choose Manna Meadows

Siri Paye is the famous meadow above Shogran, and in July and August it can be busy with jeeps and visitors. Manna Meadows offers the opposite: open grass, forest edges, streams and very few people. The trek section adds a sense of adventure, and the slower pace leaves time for photography and simply sitting in the quiet. Trekkers, photographers and anyone who has already seen Siri Paye usually love it.

What to bring

  • A warm jacket; temperatures drop sharply in the afternoon and evening
  • Walking shoes or hiking boots with good grip
  • Water, snacks and a packed lunch
  • A fully charged phone or camera and a power bank
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat
  • Cash for extras and tips; there is no ATM in Shogran

Combine it with your Shogran stay

This tour starts in Shogran, so you need a room there the night before and usually the night after. Good bases near Shogran Bazar include Pine Park Hotel and Resorts, Grand Vista Hotel and Sky View Hotel. With three nights in Shogran, you can do Siri Paye one morning and Manna Meadows the next day.

Responsible trekking on the meadows

Manna Meadows stays beautiful because few people go there. Please help keep it that way: carry all rubbish back to Shogran, stay on the paths your guide uses, keep a respectful distance from grazing animals and shepherd camps, and only light fires where your guide sets up the bonfire. Ask before photographing local herders and their families.
