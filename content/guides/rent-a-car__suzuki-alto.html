Can a Suzuki Alto drive up to Shogran?

Yes, in dry weather and with a light load. The route from Islamabad follows the Hazara Motorway (E-35) to Mansehra, then the road through Balakot and along the Kunhar River to Kiwai, about 240 km of good road. The final 7-9 km from Kiwai to Shogran is steep and narrow, with tight hairpins. A small engine with four adults and full bags works hard here, so our drivers take it slowly and stop if the brakes need to cool. With two or three passengers and soft bags, the Alto manages the climb comfortably.

The Alto is not the right car if you are travelling in winter (December to February), in heavy monsoon rain, or with four adults and big suitcases. In those cases a Suzuki Cultus, Toyota Corolla GLi or a 4×4 such as the Toyota Fortuner is a better choice.

Sample 2-night trip in the Alto

  1. Day 1: Pickup in Islamabad around 7 AM, tea at Kawai Waterfall in Kiwai, arrive in Shogran mid-afternoon.
  2. Day 2: Park the Alto at your hotel and take the Siri Paye 4×4 jeep at 7 AM; ordinary cars cannot climb the Siri Paye track.
  3. Day 3: Drive back to Islamabad, about 5-6 hours.

Packing tips for a small car

  • Use soft bags instead of hard suitcases; they fit the boot and the seat gaps more easily.
  • Pack warm jackets on top so you can reach them in Shogran, where evenings are cool even in summer.
  • Carry enough cash; there is no ATM in Shogran and the last reliable ATM is in Balakot.

How many people fit in a Suzuki Alto?

Up to four passengers. For four adults on a mountain trip, we recommend light, soft luggage, or upgrading to a Cultus or Corolla.

Is the Suzuki Alto good for winter trips to Shogran?

No. From December to February snow and ice on the climb from Kiwai call for a 4×4 such as the Fortuner or Prado, and even those depend on the road being open.

Does the Alto have air conditioning?

Yes. Our Suzuki Altos have AC and power steering, which keeps the motorway and valley sections comfortable in summer.
