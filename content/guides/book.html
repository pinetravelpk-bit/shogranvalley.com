What happens after you send your enquiry

  1. We receive your request with a reference number, so you can quote it in any message.
  2. We check availability with the hotel, driver or tour guide for your dates.
  3. We confirm on WhatsApp with the final price, what is included and any seasonal notes, such as road conditions in winter.
  4. You confirm and pay the agreed advance by cash or bank transfer.
  5. You receive your confirmation with hotel, driver or guide contact details and pickup times.

When you book a specific hotel room from its own page, you also see an itemised invoice immediately after submitting, which you can download as a PDF and send to us on WhatsApp. That is the fastest way to get a confirmed room.

Popular trip plans we arrange

Weekend in Shogran (2 nights)

Drive from Islamabad on day one (about 250 km, 5-6 hours via Mansehra, Balakot and Kiwai), take an early 4×4 jeep to Siri Paye on day two, and return on day three with a stop at Kawai Waterfall. Ideal with a room at Pine Park, Sky View or Grand Vista in Shogran.

Shogran and Sharan Forest (3 nights)

Add a night by the river in Malkandi or Paras and a jeep ride up to Sharan Forest. Fairyland Hotel and Pine Wood Hotel in Malkandi, or Parbat View in Paras, are convenient bases.

Shogran, Naran and Babusar Top (4-5 nights)

Continue about 60 km up the valley to Naran for Lake Saif-ul-Malook and, between roughly July and September, Babusar Top. A comfortable car or 7-seater with driver is the easiest way to do this route.

Winter snow trip (1-2 nights)

From December to February Shogran is usually under snow. We recommend a 4×4 and flexible dates, because heavy snowfall can close the climb from Kiwai for a day or two.

Booking tips from our local team

  • Book early for peak dates. June to August weekends, Eid holidays and snowy winter weekends sell out first.
  • Bring cash. There is no ATM in Shogran. The last reliable ATM is in Balakot.
  • Plan Siri Paye for the morning. Leave Shogran by about 7 AM, because clouds often cover the meadows by late morning.
  • Check the season. The Siri Paye jeep track is usually open from about May to October and closed by snow in winter.
  • Keep your reference. Quote your booking reference on WhatsApp so we can find your request immediately.

Group, school and corporate bookings

For groups of ten or more we plan transport, rooms and meals together, so everyone travels and stays in one place. Toyota Hiace vans carry 13-14 people and the Toyota Coaster carries up to 22, and larger groups can travel in several vehicles with drivers who keep in touch on the road. Tell us the number of rooms you need, how many people share each room, any dietary needs and whether you want a guide, bonfire or barbecue evening, and we will send one combined quote.

Companies often book Shogran for team retreats because it is close enough to Islamabad for a two-night trip, yet high enough to feel far from the city. Schools and universities usually prefer May, June and September, when the weather is mild and the Siri Paye track is open.

Travelling from overseas

If you are visiting Pakistan from abroad, fly into Islamabad International Airport and we can collect you from the airport or your Islamabad hotel. From there it is about 250 km to Shogran, usually 5-6 hours by road. Overseas Pakistanis and foreign visitors should carry their passport or CNIC, as police check posts on the Kaghan road may ask for identification. Our team can advise on the best dates around your flights and keep in touch with you on WhatsApp before you land.
