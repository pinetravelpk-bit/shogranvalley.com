Kawai (Kiwai) Waterfall guide: location, stop time and what to combine it with

Quick answer: Kawai Waterfall, also spelled Kiwai Waterfall, is a roadside waterfall at Kiwai on the Kaghan road, about 34 km from Balakot along the Kunhar River, right where the 7-9 km climb to Shogran begins. No jeep is needed and any car can stop there. Allow 30-60 minutes for photos and tea, ideally on your way up to Shogran or on the way to Naran.

For most travellers Kawai Waterfall is the first real "mountain moment" of a Kaghan Valley trip. After the drive from Balakot along the river, the road reaches Kiwai, a small settlement of tea stalls, small restaurants and parking spots. A stream tumbles down the hillside here and the spray cools the air even in the heat of June. It is not a remote trek; it is an easy, family-friendly stop that fits naturally into any trip to Shogran, Siri Paye, Sharan or Naran.

Kiwai is also the junction for Shogran. From the waterfall area a narrow road climbs steeply through pine forest to Shogran at 7,749 ft. Many drivers stop at Kiwai for tea and a rest, and to let their brakes cool, before the climb.

Why stop at Kawai Waterfall

  • Easy access. You can see the falls from the road, so it suits older travellers and small children.
  • Cool break. The spray and shade make it a welcome rest after the drive from Islamabad or Lahore.
  • Food and tea. Roadside stalls and small eateries serve tea, pakoras and simple meals.
  • Photos. The white water against green hillside is a classic Kaghan Valley photo, especially in late spring.
  • Perfect timing. It breaks the journey right before the climb to Shogran or the onward drive to Paras, Kaghan and Naran.

How to fit Kawai Waterfall into your trip

On the way to Shogran

Stop at Kiwai for 30 minutes, then drive up to Shogran to check in. If you arrive late in the afternoon, save the waterfall for the drive back so you climb to Shogran in daylight.

On a Siri Paye day

After an early jeep trip to Siri Paye, drive down from Shogran to Kiwai for lunch by the waterfall, then continue to Paras and Malkandi or return to Islamabad.

On the way to Naran

Kiwai is on the main Kaghan road, so it is a natural first stop on the way to Paras, Kaghan town and Naran, about 60 km beyond Shogran.

Safety and practical tips

  • Park only where it is safe and leave space for traffic on the narrow road.
  • Rocks near the falls are slippery; keep children close and avoid climbing on wet stones.
  • In the monsoon (July and August) the flow is strong and the road can be affected by landslides. Check conditions before travelling.
  • Carry cash in small notes. There is no ATM at Kiwai or in Shogran; the last reliable ATM is in Balakot.
  • Take your litter with you and keep the stream clean.

Is Kawai Waterfall the same as Kiwai Waterfall?

Yes. Kawai and Kiwai are two spellings of the same place on the Kaghan road below Shogran. You will see both on signs and maps.

How far is Kawai Waterfall from Shogran?

About 7-9 km. It is at the bottom of the road that climbs up to Shogran, so most visitors pass it on the way in and out.

Is there an entry fee for Kawai Waterfall?

It is a roadside stop, so there is normally no formal entry fee. You may pay for parking or food at the stalls nearby.

Can I visit Kawai Waterfall in winter?

Yes, if the Kaghan road is open. The falls can partly freeze in very cold spells, and roads can be icy, so drive carefully and check conditions first.
