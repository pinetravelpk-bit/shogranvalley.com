Which Pine Park room should you choose?

Every room has the same bed layout, so the choice comes down to building, floor and budget.

  • Standard Room (PKR 20,500): the best value for families who will spend most of the day outdoors at Siri Paye and in the forest.
  • Luxury Room (PKR 24,500): a step up in finish and furnishing for couples and families who want more comfort.
  • VIP Room, 1st floor (PKR 25,500): an upper-floor room, a good pick if you prefer more privacy and views.
  • Luxury Main Block (PKR 28,500): rooms in the main building, close to the restaurant and reception.
  • VIP/Executive, 2nd floor (PKR 28,500): the top-floor rooms, popular with honeymooners and guests who want the quietest stay.

Travelling as a group of 8 to 12? Book two or three rooms of the same type so everyone is together; our team can hold adjoining rooms when they are free.

Facilities

Pine Park lists Wi-Fi, a restaurant, room service, generator backup, hot water, heating, TV, a garden with bonfire and BBQ, laundry, parking, security, family rooms and jeep bookings, with mountain and valley views. In Shogran, power and mobile signal can drop in bad weather, so the generator backup and heating are more useful than they sound.

Sample 2-night stay at Pine Park

  1. Day 1: Leave Islamabad by 7 AM, breakfast in Abbottabad or Mansehra, withdraw cash in Balakot, tea at Kawai Waterfall, and check in from 1:00 PM. Walk in the pine forest, then dinner and a bonfire at the hotel.
  2. Day 2: Early breakfast and a Siri Paye jeep at about 7 AM, before the clouds come in. Back for lunch, then a relaxed afternoon in the garden.
  3. Day 3: Check out by noon and drive down via Kiwai, or continue up the valley to Naran, about 60 km and 2-2.5 hours away.

Best time to stay

May to June and September to October bring green meadows, clear mornings and fewer crowds. July and August are the busiest months, with monsoon showers, so book early. December to February brings snow to Shogran; the road from Kiwai can close after heavy snowfall, so travel in a 4×4 and confirm road conditions with us before you leave. Siri Paye is usually reachable from about May to October.

Is Pine Park Shogran good for families with children?

Yes. Every room sleeps up to four with a double bed and a sofa bed, and the hotel has a garden, restaurant, heating and hot water, with gentle forest walks close by.

Is Pine Park open in winter?

Shogran hotels can stay open in winter, but snow can close the road from Kiwai for a day or more. Check with our team before booking a December to February stay and travel in a 4×4.

Can Pine Park arrange the Siri Paye jeep?

Yes. Jeep bookings are listed for the hotel, and our team can add the jeep, about PKR 12,000 per jeep for the return trip, to your booking.

Which Pine Park room is best for a honeymoon?

The VIP/Executive rooms on the 2nd floor or the VIP rooms on the 1st floor, for privacy and views.
