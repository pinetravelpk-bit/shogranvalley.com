What the extra cost buys on this route

The route from Islamabad to Shogran has two very different halves. The first is about three hours of motorway and main road through Hazara: the Hazara Motorway to Mansehra, then on to Balakot. Here cruise control, a quiet cabin and climate control make the hours pass easily. The second half is the Kaghan road along the Kunhar River and the steep 7-9 km climb from Kiwai to Shogran, with hairpins all the way. An automatic gearbox and the larger 1800cc engine make that climb smoother, so passengers arrive less tired.

For families with older parents, couples on a special trip and business travellers, that comfort is often worth the difference over a Corolla GLi.

Is the Altis Grande automatic?

Yes. It has an automatic gearbox with an 1800cc petrol engine, which makes the hairpin climb from Kiwai to Shogran much smoother.

How much is the Altis Grande from Islamabad to Shogran?

About PKR 32,000 one way with driver and fuel included. The daily rate is about PKR 18,000, or about PKR 22,000 in peak season.

Is the Altis Grande worth it over a Corolla GLi?

If comfort matters, yes: automatic gearbox, climate control, cruise control and leather seats make a long mountain day easier. For pure value, the GLi is fine.

How many bags fit in the Altis Grande?

Its boot comfortably takes luggage for four people on a short trip. For more luggage or more people, choose the Honda BR-V.
