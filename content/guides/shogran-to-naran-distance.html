Getting from Shogran to Naran: transport options and prices

Quick answer: The easiest way from Shogran to Naran (about 60 km, 2-2½ hours) is a car with driver that already brought you to Shogran. If you are starting from Islamabad, one-way prices to Naran in season are about PKR 22,000 in a Suzuki Cultus, PKR 29,000 in a Toyota Corolla GLi, PKR 32,000 in a Suzuki APV and PKR 40,000 in a Honda BR-V, with driver and fuel.

Stops worth making on the way

  • Kawai Waterfall at Kiwai, at the bottom of the Shogran road
  • Paras on the Kunhar River, the turn for Sharan Forest
  • The riverside stretch around Malkandi, with hotels such as Fairyland
  • Kaghan town, a good tea and fuel stop before Naran

Can I get a jeep from Shogran to Naran?

You do not need a jeep for the main road to Naran; a normal car is fine in summer. Jeeps are only needed for side trips such as Siri Paye, Sharan Forest and Lake Saif-ul-Malook.
