1 day Shogran and Siri Paye tour from Islamabad: is a day trip worth it?

Quick answer: Yes, if you are short on time and happy with a long day. This group tour picks you up at 4:30 AM at Faizabad, Islamabad (4:45 AM at Saddar, Rawalpindi), drives about 250 km via Abbottabad, Mansehra and Balakot, switches to 4×4 jeeps at Kiwai for Shogran and Siri Paye, and drops you back around 7:30 PM. It costs about PKR 4,500 per person (minimum 4) and includes transport, jeeps, a local guide, breakfast and tolls.

How the day runs

  1. 4:30 AM: Pickup in Islamabad, then Rawalpindi.
  2. Morning: Drive via Abbottabad, Mansehra and Balakot, with a breakfast stop.
  3. Around 9 AM: Arrive at Kiwai on the Kaghan road and board 4×4 jeeps for the steep 7-9 km climb to Shogran.
  4. Late morning: Short walk, photos and tea in Shogran at 7,749 ft.
  5. Midday: Jeep ride up the rough track to the Siri Paye meadows at about 9,500 ft. Time for photos, a meadow walk or a horse ride (paid separately).
  6. Afternoon: Back to Shogran for lunch (own expense), then jeeps down to Kiwai.
  7. Around 7:30 PM: Drop-off in Islamabad and Rawalpindi.

Exact timings shift with traffic, weather and group pace; your guide keeps the day moving.

The honest trade-off: on a day trip you reach Siri Paye around midday, when clouds often build over the meadow. On an overnight trip you can go up at 7 AM for clearer views of the lakes and Makra Peak. If you can spare one night, it is usually worth it.

Who this tour suits

  • Students and friends' groups on a budget
  • Working professionals with one free day
  • Families with older children who are fine with an early start and long drive
  • Visitors to Islamabad who want a taste of the mountains

It is less suitable for very young children, anyone prone to severe car sickness, or travellers who want a relaxed pace.

What to bring

  • A warm layer: Siri Paye is much colder than Islamabad, even in summer
  • Comfortable shoes with grip for the meadow
  • Cash for lunch, tea and horse rides; there is no ATM in Shogran
  • Water, snacks and any travel-sickness medicine
  • Your CNIC for police check posts

Best season for this day trip

The Siri Paye track is usually open from about May to October, so this tour runs in that window. June often has the clearest skies, July and August are the greenest but bring afternoon rain, and September is quiet and crisp. In winter, choose our winter snow tour instead, because Siri Paye is closed by snow.
