When each part of the Kaghan Valley is open

Quick answer: Shogran can be visited all year (snow December to February), the Siri Paye jeep track is usually open about May to October, Sharan Forest is best May to September, Naran is mainly a May to October destination, and Babusar Top usually opens only about July to September or October. For a trip that includes everything, aim for July to September.

Choosing your dates for a combined trip

If you want Shogran, Siri Paye, Sharan, Naran and Babusar Top in one trip, July to early September is the only window when all of them are reliably open. Expect more visitors and some monsoon rain, so book hotels early and allow extra driving time. For a calmer Shogran and Sharan trip without Babusar, June and September are ideal.

When can I visit both Shogran and Babusar Top?

Between about July and early September, when Babusar Top is usually open and the Siri Paye track is also running.

Is the Kaghan Valley open in winter?

The lower valley, including Shogran, can be visited in winter with a 4×4, but the upper valley around Naran and Babusar Top is largely closed by snow.
