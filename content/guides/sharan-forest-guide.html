Quick answer: You can visit Sharan Forest on a guided group tour (2 days from about PKR 12,000 per person, or 3 days from Lahore from about PKR 13,000), as an overnight forest stay on our 3-day Sharan, Shogran and Siri Paye tour (about PKR 12,999 per person), or independently with a car and driver to Paras and a local 4×4 jeep up the forest track.

Where to stay near Sharan

For an easy start to the jeep track, stay in Paras at Parbat View Hotel and River Restaurant (from about PKR 8,000), which can help arrange jeeps, or by the river in Malkandi at Fairyland or Pine Wood. For more about the forest itself, read our Sharan Forest destination guide.

Is there a tour that includes a night in Sharan Forest?

Yes. Our 3-day Sharan, Shogran and Siri Paye tour from Islamabad includes one night in a Sharan hotel or cottage, for about PKR 12,999 per person.

Which hotel is closest to the Sharan jeep track?

Parbat View Hotel in Paras, where the jeep track to Sharan Forest begins.
