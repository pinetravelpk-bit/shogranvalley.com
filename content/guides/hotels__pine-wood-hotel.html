Why stay at Pine Wood?

Pine Wood is for travellers who want a simple, homely base by the river without paying Shogran or Naran prices. The rate stays the same on weekends and in peak season on our listing, which is unusual in the Kaghan Valley, where July, August and Eid rates often jump. With a double bed and a single bed in the Standard Room, a family of three or four can share one room, which keeps a trip affordable.

Malakandi sits on the main Naran road, beyond Paras and close to Mahandri, so you can explore the upper and lower valley in either direction. The garden, bonfire and barbecue evenings suit families and groups of friends, and the hotel can help arrange jeeps for side trips such as Sharan Forest.

Who Pine Wood suits

  • Budget families who want one room for four people
  • Friends' groups looking for bonfire and barbecue nights by the river
  • Road-trippers heading to Naran who want a quieter stop than Naran bazaar
  • Sharan Forest visitors who want to be close to the Paras jeep track

It is less suited to travellers who want luxury finishes, room heating in winter or a full-service resort. For those, look at Pine Park Hotel and Resorts or Grand Vista in Shogran.

Things to do from Pine Wood

  • Sharan Forest by jeep from Paras: dense conifer forest, walks and picnics
  • Malakandi forest and quiet river walks

  • Naran and Lake Saif-ul-Malook further up the valley

Tips for your stay

  • Bring warm layers; river evenings in the Kaghan Valley are cool even in summer.
  • Carry cash; the last reliable ATM is in Balakot.
  • Keep children away from the river bank; the Kunhar is fast and cold.
  • Ask about the bonfire and barbecue when you book so the hotel can prepare.
