Before-you-drive safety checklist

Quick answer: The road to Shogran is safe with sensible precautions: drive in daylight, check brakes and tyres before the Kiwai climb, avoid heavy rain and fresh snow, use low gears downhill, and consider a local driver who knows the hairpins. Our team can confirm current road conditions on WhatsApp at +92 343 5853835.

  1. Vehicle check: brakes, tyres (including the spare), lights, wipers and coolant.
  2. Weather check: avoid travelling during heavy rain or right after fresh snow.
  3. Timing: leave Islamabad by about 7 AM so the Kiwai climb is in daylight.
  4. On the climb: use low gears, keep to your side on bends, and give way to vehicles coming uphill where the road is narrow.
  5. Going down: engine braking in low gear; stop to let brakes cool if you smell them.
  6. Documents: carry your CNIC or passport for check posts.
  7. Contacts: save your hotel's number and ours before you lose signal.

Is it safe for first-time mountain drivers to drive to Shogran?

The motorway and valley road are straightforward, but the final climb from Kiwai has tight hairpins. First-time mountain drivers often feel more comfortable with a local driver for that section.

What should I do if it starts raining heavily on the way?

Slow down, avoid stopping under steep slopes, and consider waiting in Balakot or Kiwai until the rain eases before climbing to Shogran.
