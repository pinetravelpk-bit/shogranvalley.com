Your two days

Day 1: Islamabad to Shogran

Your private car collects you early in the morning and follows the Hazara route through Abbottabad and Mansehra, with time for a relaxed lunch in Balakot. At Kiwai you switch to a 4×4 jeep for the climb through pine forest to Shogran, arriving in the late afternoon. After check-in, your photographer leads a couple's photo session in the pine forest as the light softens. In the evening, a candlelight dinner can be arranged at the hotel (optional, own expense).

Day 2: Sunrise and Siri Paye

Wake early to watch the sun rise over the Kaghan Valley, then breakfast and a jeep ride up to the Siri Paye meadows at about 9,500 ft. Walk to the small lakes, ride horses across the meadow if you like, and take photos with Makra Peak behind you. Return to Shogran around lunchtime, check out, and drive back to Islamabad in the comfort of your private vehicle, arriving in the evening.

Why Shogran for a honeymoon

  • Close but far away. About 250 km and 5-6 hours from Islamabad, yet it feels like another world of pine forest and cool air.
  • Romantic settings. Forest walks, valley sunrises and bonfire evenings.
  • Siri Paye. One of the most photogenic meadows in northern Pakistan for couple portraits.
  • Privacy. A private vehicle and guide mean the trip runs at your pace.

Best season

May to October, when the Siri Paye track is open. June and September bring the clearest mornings; July and August are greenest but rainier. For a winter honeymoon in the snow, ask about a private version of our winter snow tour, since Siri Paye is closed in winter.
