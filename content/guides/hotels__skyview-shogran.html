Sky View Hotel and Family Resort Shogran: rooms, location and booking guide

Quick answer: Sky View Hotel and Family Resort is a family-friendly hotel in Shogran, near the forest house, within walking distance of the Shogran forest and about 8 km below Siri Paye. Deluxe rooms (1 double bed, up to 3 guests) cost about PKR 13,500 and Luxury rooms (1 king bed, up to 4 guests) about PKR 15,500 per night, with the same listed rate on weekends and in peak season. Breakfast, heating, hot water and a restaurant are listed, and cancellation is free up to 4 days before arrival.

Location

Sky View is on the Shogran plateau at 7,749 ft, near the forest house, with the pine forest a short walk away. Siri Paye is about 8 km above, reached by local 4×4 jeep in 45-60 minutes. From Islamabad, it is about 250 km and 5-6 hours via the Hazara Motorway, Mansehra, Balakot and Kiwai, with the last 7-9 km a steep climb from Kiwai.

Why families choose Sky View

  • In Shogran itself, so an early Siri Paye jeep is easy.
  • Heating and hot water for cool summer nights and cold shoulder seasons.
  • Breakfast and a restaurant on site, useful because Shogran has limited dining.
  • Family room sizes, with the Luxury room sleeping up to four.
  • Mid-range price between budget cottages and luxury resorts.
