Jeep and 4×4 prices on Shogran Valley

Quick answer: Local track jeeps cost about PKR 12,000 per jeep for Shogran to Siri Paye and back, about PKR 3,500 for the Kiwai to Shogran climb and about PKR 10,000 for Manna Meadows. For the whole trip from Islamabad, a Toyota Fortuner 4×4 with driver costs about PKR 32,000 per day and a Toyota Prado about PKR 35,000 per day.

Local jeep or full-trip 4×4?

Most summer travellers drive up in an ordinary car and book a local jeep only for Siri Paye. That is the cheapest combination. A full-trip 4×4 makes sense in winter snow, during heavy monsoon rain, for Babusar Top, or if you want one comfortable vehicle that can handle anything without switching.

Can I hire a jeep at Kiwai to go up to Shogran?

Yes. The Kiwai to Shogran jeep costs about PKR 3,500 per jeep, useful if you arrive by bus or your car struggles in wet weather.

Which 4×4 is best for a winter trip to Shogran?

The Toyota Fortuner is the best value, with diesel, 4×4 and seven seats. The Prado and Land Cruiser add more luxury.
