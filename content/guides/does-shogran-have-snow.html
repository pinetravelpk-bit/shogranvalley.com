Planning a snow trip to Shogran

Quick answer: For snow in Shogran, go between December and February, travel in a 4×4, book a hotel with room heating, and keep your dates flexible, because heavy snowfall can close the 7-9 km climb from Kiwai for a day or two. Our winter snow tour costs about PKR 15,000 per person with 4×4 transport, a hotel night, a bonfire and BBQ dinner and sleds.

What to wear

Nights in Shogran can drop to about -10°C in deep winter. Pack a heavy jacket, thermal base layers, waterproof gloves, a warm hat, snow boots or waterproof shoes with grip, and spare socks. Sunglasses help with snow glare on bright days.

How cold does Shogran get in winter?

Very cold. Night temperatures in deep winter can fall to around -10°C, so heated rooms and proper winter clothing are essential.

Can I combine snow in Shogran with Naran?

Not usually. The upper Kaghan Valley around Naran gets very heavy snow and most of it, including Babusar Top, is closed in winter. Plan Naran between May and October.

Is there a guided snow tour to Shogran?

Yes. Our 2-day winter snow tour from Islamabad and Rawalpindi runs from December to February for about PKR 15,000 per person, subject to road conditions.
