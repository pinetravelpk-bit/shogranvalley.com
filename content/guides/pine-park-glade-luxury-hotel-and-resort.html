Staying in Shogran: what to expect

Shogran is a hill station on a pine-covered plateau at 7,749 ft (2,362 m), about 250 km and 5-6 hours from Islamabad via the Hazara Motorway, Mansehra, Balakot and Kiwai. The last 7-9 km from Kiwai is a steep, winding climb that ordinary cars manage in dry weather. Hotels are spread around the plateau and the small bazaar, with pine forest on every side.

  • Evenings are cool, even in July, and below freezing in winter. Heating and hot water matter.
  • Power cuts are common in the valley, so generators are standard.
  • There is no ATM in Shogran; the last reliable ATM is in Balakot.
  • Siri Paye is 8-10 km above Shogran by 4×4 jeep, 45-60 minutes each way.

  1. Day 1: Drive from Islamabad, stop at Kawai Waterfall in Kiwai, check in by mid-afternoon.
  2. Day 2: Leave at 7 AM in a Siri Paye jeep for the clearest views of the lakes and Makra Peak, back by lunch, then a bonfire evening.
  3. Day 3: Return to Islamabad, or continue to Paras, Sharan Forest and Naran.
