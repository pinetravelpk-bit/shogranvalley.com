An honest look at the Bolan

The Bolan has carried Pakistani families and their luggage for decades. It is simple, tough and cheap to run, with a boxy shape that swallows bags and a rear door that makes loading easy. For students, friends and budget families it turns a Shogran trip from expensive to affordable, because seven people share one vehicle and one driver.

The trade-offs are real. Without AC, the hottest sections, Islamabad to Mansehra in June, can be warm, so early starts help. The ride is firm on the rough patches between Balakot and Kiwai. And seven adults with luggage is a heavy load for a small engine on the 7-9 km climb to Shogran, so the driver will take it slowly in low gear. In cooler months (May, September, October) and with a mixed group of adults and children, the Bolan works very well.

Money-saving plan: bus to Balakot, Bolan to Shogran

If your group is already travelling to Balakot by public transport, you can book only the short Balakot to Shogran transfer for about PKR 6,000. The driver meets you in Balakot, follows the Kaghan road about 34 km to Kiwai and climbs the last stretch to Shogran. It is the cheapest door-to-door option for groups.

Sample budget weekend for seven

  1. Day 1: 5 AM start from Islamabad to beat the heat, breakfast in Mansehra, tea at Kawai Waterfall, arrive in Shogran by early afternoon.
  2. Day 2: Share one Siri Paye jeep (priced per jeep, not per person) at 7 AM.
  3. Day 3: Return to Islamabad.

Pair it with a budget room at Pine Wood Hotel or Parbat View Hotel from about PKR 8,000 per night.

Does the Suzuki Bolan have AC?

Most Bolans do not have air conditioning. If you are travelling in June or July, start early, or choose the Suzuki APV, which has AC and seats eight.

How much is a Bolan from Islamabad to Shogran?

About PKR 20,000 one way with driver and fuel included. The daily rate is about PKR 9,000, or about PKR 11,000 in peak season.

Can a Bolan climb to Shogran with 7 people?

Yes, in dry weather, but slowly. Our drivers use low gears on the climb from Kiwai. With heavy luggage or in rain, a larger van is more comfortable.

Can I book only Balakot to Shogran?

Yes. The Balakot to Shogran transfer costs about PKR 6,000 per vehicle.
