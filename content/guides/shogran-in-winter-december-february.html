Booking a winter stay in Shogran

Quick answer: A good winter trip to Shogran combines a 4×4 with driver, a heated hotel room and flexible dates. On Shogran Valley you can book a Toyota Fortuner from about PKR 32,000 per day, a room at Pine Park Hotel and Resorts (heating listed) from about PKR 20,500 per night, or the all-in winter snow tour for about PKR 15,000 per person.

A sample winter weekend

  1. Friday: We confirm the road the day before. Leave Islamabad early, lunch in Balakot, climb from Kiwai in daylight.
  2. Saturday: Snow walks in the pine forest, sledging and photos, a bonfire and hot dinner in the evening.
  3. Sunday: Slow breakfast, descend before midday, back in Islamabad by evening.

Winter extras to ask about

  • Extra blankets or a room heater for children
  • Generator hours, so you can charge phones and cameras
  • Hot meals at the hotel, since fewer restaurants open in winter
  • Sleds for snow play, which our winter tour includes

What is the cheapest way to see Shogran in winter?

Our 2-day winter snow tour, at about PKR 15,000 per person, includes 4×4 transport, a hotel night, a bonfire and BBQ dinner and sleds.

Which Shogran hotels have heating?

Pine Park Hotel and Resorts and Sky View Hotel and Family Resort list room heating and hot water. Always confirm heating when you book in winter.
