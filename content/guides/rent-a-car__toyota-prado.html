Why the Prado is worth it in the Kaghan Valley

Every comfortable car can reach Shogran on a dry summer day. The Prado matters on the other days. In heavy rain, the steep 7-9 km climb from Kiwai can be muddy and slippery, and four-wheel drive turns a nervous section into a routine one. In winter, from December to February, snow and ice on that same climb rule out two-wheel-drive cars entirely; a Prado with an experienced driver is one of the few comfortable ways up, on the days the road is open.

The Prado also has the ground clearance and suspension for rough stretches between Balakot and Kiwai after the monsoon, and the comfort to make a 5-6 hour drive from Islamabad feel easy for seven people.   • Families of up to seven who want comfort and safety in any weather.
  • VIP guests and executives visiting the valley.
  • Monsoon travellers (July and August), when rain makes the climb harder.
  • Longer routes to Naran, and to Babusar Top when the pass is open.

Winter travel in a Prado: what to expect

  1. We check road status at Kiwai the day before and the morning of travel.
  2. After fresh heavy snow, the climb may close for a day or two until it is cleared; we will advise you to wait or change dates.
  3. Drivers slow right down on ice and packed snow; ask us whether snow chains are needed for your dates.
  4. Pack thermal layers; Shogran nights in winter are well below freezing.

Even the best 4×4 cannot drive a closed road. Flexible dates are the key to a good winter trip.

How much is a Prado with driver from Islamabad to Shogran?

About PKR 55,000 one way with driver and fuel included. The daily rate is about PKR 35,000, or about PKR 42,000 in peak season.

Is a Prado necessary for Shogran in winter?

A 4×4 such as the Prado is strongly recommended from December to February because of snow and ice on the climb from Kiwai. The road can still close after heavy snowfall.

How many people fit in a Prado?

Up to seven passengers. With full luggage, five or six is the most comfortable.

Can the Prado go to Babusar Top?

Yes, when the pass is open, usually from about July to September or October. Check with our team before planning.
