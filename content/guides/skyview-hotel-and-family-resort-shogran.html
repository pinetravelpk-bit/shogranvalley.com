Why families choose Skyview

Skyview is a practical mid-range choice for families who want to sleep in Shogran itself. Being on the plateau means an easy 7 AM start for Siri Paye, when the light is best and before clouds roll in. Heating and hot water make cool evenings comfortable, the restaurant saves searching for dinner, and the Luxury room's king bed works for a couple with one or two young children.
