Why travellers choose the Corolla

The Corolla is the most common car on Pakistani roads, which says a lot about its reliability and how easy it is to service anywhere, including in Balakot or Naran if anything is ever needed. On a 5-6 hour journey from Islamabad to Shogran, that dependability matters more than extra gadgets.

On the Hazara Motorway the Corolla cruises quietly, and on the Mansehra to Balakot road it rides well over the uneven patches. On the climb from Kiwai, the 1300cc engine pulls four adults steadily up the hairpins. The large boot takes proper suitcases, so families do not have to pack in soft bags.

Who should book the Corolla GLi?

  • Families of four who want a comfortable, familiar car
  • Business travellers heading to meetings or events in the Kaghan Valley
  • Couples on a longer trip that includes Naran and Lake Saif-ul-Malook
  • Travellers flying into Islamabad who want an airport pickup straight to Shogran

When to choose something else

From December to February, snow and ice on the Kiwai climb mean a 4×4 like the Toyota Fortuner or Prado is the safer option. For five to seven people, the Honda BR-V gives seven seats and more ground clearance. And for Siri Paye, every car stays at the hotel while you take a local 4×4 jeep.

Sample 2-night itinerary

  1. Day 1: Islamabad pickup, motorway to Mansehra, lunch near Balakot, Kawai Waterfall, check in at your Shogran hotel.
  2. Day 2: 7 AM jeep to Siri Paye, afternoon walk in Shogran's pine forest.
  3. Day 3: Return to Islamabad, arriving by evening.

Can a Corolla reach Shogran?

Yes, in dry weather. The climb from Kiwai is steep with hairpins but a Corolla with an experienced driver manages it well. In snow, choose a 4×4.
