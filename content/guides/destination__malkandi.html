Malkandi (Malakandi) Forest and village guide

Quick answer: Malkandi, also spelled Malakandi, is a forested riverside area of the Kaghan Valley in Mansehra District, reached by following the Kaghan road up the valley through Paras. It is a quiet alternative base to Shogran and Naran, with riverside hotels such as Fairyland Hotel and Restaurant and Pine Wood Hotel and Family Cottage, and it pairs naturally with a day in Sharan Forest. The best season is May to September.

Most visitors to the Kaghan Valley rush between Shogran and Naran. Malkandi rewards those who slow down: forested slopes run down to the Kunhar River, nights are calm, and hotels are smaller and more family-run. If you want river sounds instead of a busy bazaar, Malkandi is worth a night or two.

How to get to Malkandi

From Islamabad

Take the Hazara Motorway to Mansehra, continue to Balakot and follow the Kaghan road along the Kunhar River past Kiwai to Paras and on to Malkandi. Allow around 6 hours in normal traffic. The road is paved and suitable for ordinary cars in dry weather.

From Shogran

Drive down the 7-9 km road from Shogran to Kiwai, then continue up the main valley road through Paras. It is an easy half-day move, so many travellers spend their first nights in Shogran for Siri Paye and then move to Malkandi for the forest and river.

Things to do around Malkandi

  • Sharan Forest day trip. Take a 4×4 jeep from Paras up through dense conifer forest to the Sharan rest area at about 2,500 m.
  • River evenings. Sit by the Kunhar with tea, or enjoy a bonfire and barbecue at your hotel.
  • Forest walks. Short walks on quiet forest paths near the village.
  • Shogran and Siri Paye. Drive back to Kiwai and up to Shogran, then take a jeep to the Siri Paye meadows.
  • Naran. Continue up the valley to Naran for Lake Saif-ul-Malook and, in summer, Babusar Top.

Suggested 3-night plan with Malkandi

  1. Day 1: Islamabad to Shogran (about 250 km, 5-6 hours), with a stop at Kawai Waterfall.
  2. Day 2: Early jeep to Siri Paye, back by lunch, then drive down to Malkandi for a riverside evening.
  3. Day 3: Jeep from Paras to Sharan Forest, forest walks and a picnic, back to Malkandi.
  4. Day 4: Return to Islamabad, or continue to Naran.

Practical tips

  • Carry cash. There is no ATM in Shogran or Malkandi; the last reliable ATM is in Balakot.
  • River banks are slippery and the current is strong; keep children well away from the water.
  • Bring a warm layer. Evenings by the river are cool even in summer.
  • In July and August, monsoon rain can affect the Kaghan road, so allow extra travel time.

Is Malkandi the same as Malakandi?

Yes. Malkandi and Malakandi are two spellings of the same area in the Kaghan Valley. Hotels and maps use both.

Is Malkandi better than Shogran for a stay?

It is different rather than better. Shogran is higher, cooler and the base for Siri Paye. Malkandi is by the river, quieter and closer to Sharan Forest. Many travellers stay in both.

Do I need a jeep to reach Malkandi?

No. Malkandi is reached along the main Kaghan road, which is suitable for normal cars in dry weather. You only need a jeep for side trips such as Sharan Forest or Siri Paye.
