Built for trips with gear

Most SUVs fill up quickly once you add tents, sleeping bags, cooking kit, tripods or drone cases. The Hilux keeps people and gear separate: five travellers ride in an air-conditioned double cabin, while the open bed carries bulky or muddy equipment that no one wants inside. For a camping night in Sharan Forest, a photography trip to Siri Paye at dawn or a trekking group heading for Manna Meadows, that matters.

Underneath, it is a serious 4×4. The diesel engine pulls strongly on the climb from Kiwai, the clearance handles broken road after the monsoon, and four-wheel drive gives traction on wet or snowy surfaces. Our route list even includes a Hilux price for the Shogran to Siri Paye run, about PKR 11,000 return with waiting time; ask our team whether the Hilux or a local jeep suits the track best on your dates.

Packing the cargo bed

  • Bring waterproof covers or bags; mountain weather changes quickly and the bed is open.
  • Strap down loads; the Kiwai climb and the valley road have tight bends.
  • Keep jackets, water and cameras in the cabin so you can reach them on the way.
  • Tell us your load in advance so the driver brings ropes and covers.
