Why a BR-V for a Shogran family trip

The Honda BR-V is one of the most practical cars for families heading into the Kaghan Valley. It fits up to seven people, which usually means the whole family travels together instead of splitting into two cars. The automatic gearbox makes the steep 7-9 km climb from Kiwai to Shogran smooth, with no constant gear changes on the hairpins, and the higher ground clearance helps over the rough and broken patches that appear on the Balakot to Kiwai stretch after the monsoon. Leather seats are easy to keep clean after muddy walks and wet days in the mountains.

Things to know before booking

  • The BR-V is not a 4×4. From December to February, snow on the Kiwai climb calls for a Fortuner or Prado.
  • Cars cannot drive the Siri Paye or Sharan Forest tracks; local jeeps do those trips.
  • Carry cash. There is no ATM in Shogran, and the last reliable ATM is in Balakot.
  • Book a week or more ahead for June to August and Eid holidays.

Mountain travel tips for families

  • Start early. Leaving Islamabad between 6 and 7 AM avoids the worst of the Mansehra and Balakot traffic and gets you up the Kiwai climb in daylight.
  • Plan for motion sickness. The valley road and the hairpins above Kiwai can upset children. Sit them by a window, keep snacks light and ask the driver for short breaks.
  • Layer up. It can be hot in Islamabad and cool in Shogran on the same day. Keep jackets inside the car, not buried in the boot.
  • Keep water and snacks handy. Roadside stops are good in Mansehra, Balakot and Kiwai, but there are long stretches between them.
  • Respect the weather. In heavy rain the driver may wait out a downpour before the climb. It is the safe choice.
