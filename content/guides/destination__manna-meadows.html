Manna Meadows guide: the quiet alternative to Siri Paye

Quick answer: Manna Meadows is a quiet high pasture near Shogran in the Kaghan Valley, surrounded by pine forest and mountain views. It is visited as a full-day jeep and trek trip starting from Shogran Bazar, best between May and September. It gets far fewer visitors than Siri Paye, so it suits trekkers, photographers and anyone looking for solitude. Our guided Manna Meadows Explorer tour costs about PKR 8,000 per person (minimum 2).

Siri Paye is the meadow everyone has heard of. Manna Meadows is the one local guides take friends to when they want peace and quiet. There are no rows of jeeps or busy tea stalls; instead you get open grass, birdsong, grazing animals and long views across the forested ridges of the Kaghan Valley. It takes more effort to reach, which is exactly why it stays calm.

How a Manna Meadows day works

  1. 8:00 AM: Pickup from your hotel in Shogran.
  2. 8:30 AM: Jeep drive along the forest track towards the Manna trail.
  3. Morning: Start the trek on foot through pine forest to the open meadow.
  4. Midday: Picnic lunch, photography and free time on the meadow.
  5. Afternoon: Walk back to the jeep and return to Shogran before evening.

The exact timings depend on the weather, the group's pace and track conditions, which your guide confirms the evening before.

With two or three nights in Shogran, do both: Siri Paye early on your first full morning and Manna Meadows the next day.

What to bring

  • Walking shoes or boots with good grip; forest paths can be muddy
  • A warm layer and a light rain jacket; weather changes quickly at altitude
  • Water, snacks and a packed lunch (your hotel can prepare one)
  • Sunscreen, a hat and sunglasses
  • Cash for the jeep and tips, since there is no ATM in Shogran
  • A bag for all your rubbish

Is Manna Meadows right for you?

Choose Manna Meadows if you enjoy walking, want to avoid crowds or have already seen Siri Paye. It is less suitable for very young children, people with limited mobility or anyone short of time, because of the trek and the full-day commitment. In that case, Siri Paye by jeep is the easier choice.

Where to stay

How do you get to Manna Meadows from Shogran?

The trip starts at Shogran Bazar with a jeep ride along a forest track, followed by a trek on foot to the meadow. It takes a full day, so leave around 8 AM.

When is the best time to visit Manna Meadows?

May to September, when the tracks are open and the grass is green. June and September usually have the clearest weather.

Can children do the Manna Meadows trek?

Older children who are used to walking usually manage well. For very young children, Siri Paye by jeep is an easier option.
