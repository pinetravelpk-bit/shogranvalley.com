Grand Cabin or Hiroof?

The difference is seating comfort versus capacity. The Grand Cabin uses individual reclining seats with proper spacing instead of bench rows, which changes a 5-6 hour drive completely: passengers arrive in Shogran able to enjoy the evening rather than needing to recover. The Hiace Hiroof fits one more person and more luggage, with standing headroom, at a slightly lower price.

Driving the Kaghan road in a van

From Islamabad the Grand Cabin follows the Hazara Motorway to Mansehra, then Balakot and the Kaghan road along the Kunhar River to Kiwai. The diesel engine's low-down pull suits the 7-9 km climb to Shogran with a full group on board, and our drivers know the hairpins and the passing places on the narrow road. Plan on 5-6 hours plus meal stops. Siri Paye is still a jeep trip: a group of 12 usually needs two Siri Paye jeeps.

Sample 3-day group itinerary

  1. Day 1: Islamabad to Shogran, stops in Mansehra and at Kawai Waterfall, group dinner and bonfire.
  2. Day 2: Early Siri Paye jeeps, then a drive to Paras for Sharan Forest or a relaxed afternoon in Shogran.
  3. Day 3: Return via Balakot to Islamabad.

The 3-day Kaghan Valley vehicle package at about PKR 95,000 covers this kind of plan with the driver; your quote confirms fuel, tolls and the driver's stay.

Group booking tips

  • Send the number of travellers, the number of rooms and any older or very young passengers.
  • Agree one pickup point in Islamabad or Rawalpindi to save time.
  • Book rooms and the van together so check-in matches your arrival.
  • In winter, ask us about road status; vans are not suitable on snow.

Can a Hiace drive up to Shogran?

Yes, in dry weather. Our drivers regularly take Hiace vans up the climb from Kiwai. In snow, vans should not attempt the climb.

Is diesel cheaper for long trips?

Generally yes. The Grand Cabin's diesel engine is economical on long valley drives, which helps keep group prices down.
