Plan it from Shogran

Babusar is not a sensible day trip from Shogran. Spend a night in Naran, about 60 km from Shogran, then drive to Babusar Top early the next morning via Batakundi and Lulusar Lake, and return to Naran or continue towards Chilas. Always confirm that the pass is open before you set off; it usually opens only from about July to September or October.

Is there a tour from Islamabad that includes Babusar Top?

Yes. Our Naran Kaghan tour visits Lulusar Lake and Babusar Top on Day 2, in season, for about PKR 20,000 per person.

Which vehicle is best for Babusar Top?

A 4×4 with high clearance such as the Toyota Fortuner, Prado or Land Cruiser V8, driven by someone experienced on mountain roads.
