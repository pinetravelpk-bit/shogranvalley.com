Siri Paye day-bag checklist and horse ride tips

Quick answer: For Siri Paye, carry a warm layer, shoes with grip, sunscreen and sunglasses, water, a charged phone or camera and small cash notes for tea, horse rides and tips. Agree the horse ride price and route before you mount, and keep rides short with young children.

Horse rides on the meadow

  • Agree the price first, including how long the ride lasts and where it goes.
  • Ask for a handler to lead the horse for children and nervous riders.
  • Keep it short with young children; 10-15 minutes is plenty at altitude.
  • Avoid steep slopes and wet ground near the lakes.
  • Tip the handler if the ride was good; it is a small, seasonal income.

Are horse rides at Siri Paye safe for children?

Generally yes, if a handler leads the horse at walking pace and the ride is short. Agree the price and route before starting.

What should I wear to Siri Paye in summer?

Layers: a T-shirt or shirt with a fleece or warm jacket on top, comfortable shoes with grip and a sun hat. The meadow is much cooler and windier than Shogran.
