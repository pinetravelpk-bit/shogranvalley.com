The three days

Day 1: Departure from Lahore and Islamabad

The coach leaves Lahore and collects Islamabad travellers at Faizabad Interchange in the early morning. The route follows the Hazara Motorway to Mansehra, then Balakot and the Kunhar River into the Kaghan Valley. Depending on timing, the first jeep trip into Sharan Forest from Paras can begin on arrival, before check-in for the night.

Day 2: Shogran and Siri Paye

Jeeps climb from Kiwai to Shogran at 7,749 ft and on to the Siri Paye meadows at about 9,500 ft. This is the highlight: small lakes, wide pastures and Makra Peak, captured by the DSLR photographer and drone. Evening dinner is included.

Day 3: Kawai Waterfall and home

After breakfast, stop at Kawai Waterfall in Kiwai for photos, then drive back to Islamabad and Lahore, arriving late in the evening.

Travelling from Lahore: what to expect

Lahore to Shogran is about 600 km. The drive takes roughly 9-10 hours to the valley, so Day 1 and Day 3 involve long stretches on the coach. Bring a neck pillow, water and snacks, and expect rest stops on the motorway and in Hazara. Islamabad travellers join several hours into the journey and have a much shorter ride.

Practical tips

  • Bring warm layers; Siri Paye and Sharan are much colder than Lahore, even in June.
  • Carry cash for lunches and extras; there is no ATM in Shogran.
  • Pack light; jeeps have limited space for luggage on the mountain tracks.
  • Tell us about elderly travellers, children or health conditions in advance.
  • Monsoon rain in July and August can delay roads; the guide adjusts the plan.

Best months for this tour

The Siri Paye track and Sharan Forest are usually accessible from about May to October. June and September offer the clearest views, while July and August are the greenest and busiest. In winter, Siri Paye and Sharan are closed by snow, so we run a separate winter snow tour instead.
