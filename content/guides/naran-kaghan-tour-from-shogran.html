A 5-day Shogran and Naran plan

  1. Day 1: Islamabad to Shogran via Kawai Waterfall.
  2. Day 2: Siri Paye at dawn, afternoon in Shogran's forest.
  3. Day 3: Drive to Naran via Paras and Kaghan town, afternoon at Lake Saif-ul-Malook by local jeep.
  4. Day 4: Lulusar Lake and Babusar Top (about July to September), or a slow day in Naran.
  5. Day 5: Return to Islamabad.

How much is a Naran Kaghan tour package?

Our 3-day, 2-night Naran Kaghan tour costs about PKR 20,000 per person, including AC transport, two nights at Pine Park Hotel Naran, a Saif-ul-Malook jeep and selected meals.

Can I continue from Shogran to Naran in my rental car?

Yes. The road from Shogran via Kiwai and Paras to Naran is about 60 km and suits ordinary cars in summer. Tell us your full route when booking.
