Day by day

Night 0 and Day 1: Lahore to Naran via Balakot and Paras

The coach leaves Lahore late at night and drives up the motorway, collecting Islamabad travellers on the way. By morning the group is in the Kaghan Valley, following the Kunhar River through Balakot. Breakfast is by the river at Paras, then the drive continues past Kaghan town to Naran. After check-in at Pine Park Hotel, local jeeps take the group up to Lake Saif-ul-Malook, the famous alpine lake ringed by snow peaks. Dinner is chicken biryani.

Day 2: Lulusar Lake and Babusar Top

The road beyond Naran climbs through Batakundi to Lulusar Lake, a long turquoise lake, and on to Babusar Top at 13,690 ft, the high pass towards Chilas and Gilgit-Baltistan, with sweeping mountain views. Back in Naran, the evening ends with a live BBQ, bonfire and music.

Day 3: Naran to Lahore

After a final look at Naran, the group drives back down the valley and the motorway, dropping Islamabad travellers on the way and reaching Lahore at night.

When does this tour run?

Naran is a summer destination. Babusar Top is usually open only from about July to September or October, depending on snow, and the upper valley is quiet or closed in winter. If the pass is closed on your date, the guide adjusts Day 2 to other sights around Naran. June and September offer clearer skies, while July and August are the busiest weeks.

Tips for the overnight departure

  • Bring a neck pillow, blanket or shawl, and eye mask for sleeping on the coach.
  • Wear layers: Lahore is warm, but Babusar Top can be cold and windy even in August.
  • Pack a small day bag for the lake and pass visits.
  • Carry cash; ATMs in the upper valley are not always reliable. Withdraw in Lahore, Islamabad or Balakot.
  • Go slowly at Babusar Top; the altitude is high, so drink water and avoid rushing.
