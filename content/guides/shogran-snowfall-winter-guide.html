Winter trip checklist: before you leave for snowy Shogran

Quick answer: Before a winter trip, check the Kiwai to Shogran road the day before and the morning you leave, travel in a 4×4 with an experienced driver, confirm your hotel's heating and generator, carry enough cash from Balakot, and keep a spare day in case fresh snow closes the climb. Siri Paye is closed in winter, so plan your snow fun on the Shogran plateau.

  1. Two weeks before: book a heated room and a 4×4. Winter weekends and holidays fill up.
  2. The day before: message us or your hotel for the latest snowfall and road update.
  3. Morning of travel: leave Islamabad early so you reach Kiwai with plenty of daylight.
  4. In Balakot: withdraw cash and buy any last-minute snacks or warm gear.
  5. At Kiwai: follow your driver's advice; if the climb is closed, wait in the valley rather than force it.
  6. In Shogran: keep phones charged during generator hours and dress in layers.

Snow safety for families

  • Keep children away from slopes above the road and from roof edges where snow can slide.
  • Change out of wet clothes quickly; cold and damp is what makes people ill.
  • Walk carefully on packed snow and ice around the bazaar, especially in the morning.
  • Do not drive the climb from Kiwai after dark in winter.

What should I check before driving to Shogran in winter?

Whether the road from Kiwai to Shogran is open, whether your hotel has heating and power backup, and the snowfall forecast. Our team can confirm the latest status on WhatsApp.

Is it safe to take children to Shogran in the snow?

Yes, with sensible care: a 4×4 with an experienced driver, a heated room, proper winter clothing and close supervision near slopes and roads.
