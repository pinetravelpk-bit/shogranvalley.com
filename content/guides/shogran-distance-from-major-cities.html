Best way to reach Shogran from each city

Quick answer: From Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar and Abbottabad, drive straight to Shogran in one day. From Lahore (about 600 km), either drive 9-10 hours or join a 3-day group tour that leaves Lahore and picks up in Islamabad. From Karachi and other far cities, fly to Islamabad International Airport and continue by road, about 5-6 hours.

Breaking a long journey

If you are driving from Lahore or further, consider a night in Islamabad or Abbottabad so you tackle the Kaghan road and the steep Kiwai climb fresh and in daylight. Balakot is the last town with reliable ATMs and a range of restaurants before Shogran, so plan a stop there. Our team can arrange an airport or city pickup with driver from Islamabad so you do not have to drive the mountain section yourself.

What is the nearest airport to Shogran?

Islamabad International Airport is the practical gateway. From there Shogran is a road trip of about 5-6 hours via the Hazara Motorway, Mansehra, Balakot and Kiwai.

Can I reach Shogran from Lahore in one day?

Yes, with an early start: about 600 km and 9-10 hours of driving. Many travellers prefer a group tour or a night in Islamabad on the way.
