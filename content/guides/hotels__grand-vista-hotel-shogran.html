What to expect from your room

Grand Vista keeps things simple: one room type, built for two. Rooms are clean and spacious, with large windows that frame the pine-covered hills, so you wake up to forest and mountain light. Hot water runs around the clock with generator backup, and room service is available when you would rather stay in after a long day at Siri Paye.

Because every room is a double, Grand Vista works best for couples. Families and groups can book more than one room, and family rooms are listed on request; ask our team what is free for your dates.

Sample 2-night stay for couples

  1. Day 1: Leave Islamabad early, stop for breakfast in Mansehra, withdraw cash in Balakot (there is no ATM in Shogran), and check in from 1:00 PM. Spend the afternoon on a forest walk and the evening by the bonfire.
  2. Day 2: Breakfast, then a Siri Paye jeep at about 7 AM for the clearest views. Back for a late lunch and a quiet evening.
  3. Day 3: Check out by noon and stop at Kawai Waterfall on the way down.

Getting there

Drive the Hazara Motorway to Mansehra, then continue through Balakot to Kiwai, where a steep 7-9 km road climbs to Shogran. In dry weather any healthy car manages; in rain or snow, choose a 4×4 or park at Kiwai and take a local jeep up for about PKR 3,500. Prefer not to drive? Book a car with a driver from Islamabad or Lahore with your room.
