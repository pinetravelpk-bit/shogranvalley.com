Route and rhythm

Day 1: Lahore to Islamabad, Paras and Sharan Forest

The tour starts from Lahore and picks up Islamabad travellers on the way north. After the Hazara Motorway to Mansehra and the road through Balakot, the group reaches Paras on the Kunhar River, where jeeps take over for the steep track up to Sharan Forest. Sharan is one of the quietest pine forests in the Kaghan Valley, ideal for slow walks and starry nights.

Day 2: Shogran and Siri Paye

Jeeps bring the group to the Shogran plateau at 7,749 ft for valley views, then up the rough track to Siri Paye, where green pastures open beneath Makra Peak. The jeep rides are part of the adventure, not just transfers.

Day 3: Shogran market and the road home

A slower morning in Shogran's small market for tea and souvenirs, then the drive back via Balakot and the motorway to Islamabad and Lahore.

Why Sharan comes first

Starting with Sharan on Day 1 makes good use of the afternoon: Paras is on the main valley road, so the group reaches the forest without backtracking. Doing Siri Paye on Day 2 morning then gives the best chance of clear meadow views before afternoon clouds build. It is a well-balanced plan that avoids rushing.

Weather and delays

Mountain roads can be affected by landslides, roadblocks and heavy rain, especially in July and August. The guide may change the order of visits to keep everyone safe, and costs caused by such delays are not included in the price. Travelling between May and June or in September usually means fewer disruptions.
