If everything is open

Book your room and a Siri Paye jeep together, plan to leave Islamabad by 7 AM, and carry enough cash from Balakot, since Shogran has no ATM.

Is Sharan Forest open now?

Sharan Forest is usually accessible from about May to September. Snow and mud can close the jeep track outside that window, so ask us for the current status.

Are Shogran hotels open all year?

Most operate in the main season, and several stay open in winter for snow trips. Ask us which hotels are open and heated for your dates.
