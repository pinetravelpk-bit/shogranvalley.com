Siri Paye jeep prices and a perfect morning plan

Quick answer: The jeep from Shogran to Siri Paye costs about PKR 12,000 per jeep for the return trip with waiting time (about PKR 15,000 in peak season), not per person, and one jeep carries up to 6 people. Leave Shogran at 6:30-7 AM; the 8-10 km ride takes 45-60 minutes each way, so you are on the meadow before late-morning clouds roll in.

Splitting the cost

Because the price is per jeep, the cost per person drops quickly: a full jeep of six shares about PKR 12,000, roughly PKR 2,000 each. Larger groups simply book two or three jeeps. On our multi-day tours, the Siri Paye jeep is usually already included.

Is the Siri Paye jeep priced per person?

No, per jeep. About PKR 12,000 covers the return trip with waiting time for up to six people.

Can I book the Siri Paye jeep the night before?

Often yes, through your hotel or our team, but in July, August and holidays it is safer to book it together with your room.
