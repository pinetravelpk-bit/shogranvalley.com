The Land Cruiser on the Kaghan road

With a V8 engine, the climb from Kiwai to Shogran is simply not an event. Where smaller vehicles work hard on the steep hairpins, the Land Cruiser walks up, fully loaded, without strain. That reserve of power and traction matters most in the conditions that trouble everything else: snow and ice in winter, mud after monsoon rain, and the long, high climb from Naran past Batakundi and Lulusar Lake to Babusar Top at around 13,700 ft.

Inside, it is a quiet, insulated cabin with leather seats and a sunroof, so a long valley day ends with passengers who still feel fresh. For VIP guests, senior officials, foreign delegations and families who want the best, it is the obvious choice.

Signature routes

Islamabad to Babusar Top in a day (July to September)

Leave Islamabad at dawn, follow the Kaghan road via Balakot, Kiwai, Paras and Naran, then climb through Batakundi past Lulusar Lake to Babusar Top. It is a long day, best broken with a night in Naran or Shogran. Check that the pass is open before booking.

Shogran and Naran in comfort

Two nights in Shogran at Pine Park Hotel and Resorts or Grand Vista, then two in Naran with Lake Saif-ul-Malook. The Land Cruiser makes the transfers effortless.

Winter snow in Shogran

From December to February, when the road is open, the Land Cruiser is the most capable and comfortable way up the snowy Kiwai climb.
