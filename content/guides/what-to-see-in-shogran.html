What is a good 3-day plan for Shogran?

Day 1 drive from Islamabad via Kawai Waterfall, Day 2 an early Siri Paye jeep, Day 3 Sharan Forest or Manna Meadows before heading home.

Can I add Naran to a Shogran trip?

Yes. Naran is about 60 km beyond Shogran, so a fourth day lets you visit Lake Saif-ul-Malook and, in summer, Babusar Top.
