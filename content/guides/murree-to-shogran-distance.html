A Murree and Shogran combined itinerary

Quick answer: To see both, spend one night in Murree and two in Shogran. Drive from Murree to Shogran (about 180-200 km, 4½-5½ hours) via Abbottabad, Mansehra, Balakot and Kiwai, without going back to Islamabad. Shogran (7,749 ft) sits a little higher than Murree and feels quieter and more forested, with the Siri Paye meadows above it.

Getting around

The easiest way to do Murree and Shogran together is a car with driver for the whole trip, so the car can wait in Murree and continue north the next day. For families of five to seven, the Honda BR-V or Suzuki APV is comfortable on both the Murree hills and the Kiwai climb.

Do I need to go back to Islamabad between Murree and Shogran?

No. Drive from Murree down towards Abbottabad and join the route north via Mansehra and Balakot to Kiwai and Shogran.

How many days do I need for Murree and Shogran?

Four days is comfortable: one night in Murree, two in Shogran, and a travel day back to Islamabad.
