Why stay in Paras?

Paras is a small bazaar town on the Kaghan road, past Kiwai on the way to Naran. It is the starting point for the jeep track up to Sharan Forest, so staying here saves time if Sharan is on your list. Being on the main road also makes Paras a practical stop for families driving on to Malakandi, Kaghan town and Naran, or back down to Shogran and Kawai Waterfall.

Parbat View is right on the river, so the restaurant and rooms look over the Kunhar, with Malika Parbat in view on clear days. Listed facilities include Wi-Fi, parking, a restaurant, room service, a generator, bonfire and barbecue, mountain and valley views, and jeep arrangements.
