Fairyland Hotel and Restaurant Malakandi: river-view rooms in the Kaghan Valley

Quick answer: Fairyland Hotel and Restaurant is a 3-star river-view hotel with 25 rooms on Naran Road, Malakandi (Malkandi), Kaghan Valley. Deluxe rooms cost about PKR 12,000 per night (about PKR 14,000 on weekends and in peak season) and sleep up to 4 guests with a double bed. Facilities include Wi-Fi, parking, a restaurant, room service, a generator, hot water, TV, a garden, bonfire and barbecue. Cancellation is free up to 48 hours before arrival.

What makes Fairyland a good base

Fairyland sits by the river in Malakandi, a quiet stretch of the Kaghan Valley between Paras and Kaghan town. It is a calmer alternative to staying in Naran bazaar, yet close enough to reach Naran, Sharan Forest and Shogran as day trips. With 25 rooms, a restaurant on site and hot water, it handles families and small groups comfortably. Evenings tend to revolve around the garden, the sound of the Kunhar River and bonfire or barbecue nights, which the hotel can prepare if you ask in advance.

Who Fairyland suits

  • Families who want a 3-star hotel by the river rather than a cottage
  • Couples looking for a quiet stop between Shogran and Naran
  • Groups planning a Sharan Forest day from Paras
  • Travellers heading towards Manoor Valley, whose turn-off is nearby

A 3-night plan with Fairyland

  1. Night 1 in Shogran: drive from Islamabad (about 250 km, 5-6 hours), stop at Kawai Waterfall.
  2. Day 2: early Siri Paye jeep, then drive down to Kiwai and up the valley to Fairyland for a riverside evening.
  3. Day 3: jeep from Paras to Sharan Forest, back to Fairyland for a bonfire.
  4. Day 4: continue to Naran and Lake Saif-ul-Malook, or return to Islamabad.

Good to know

  • Withdraw cash in Balakot; there are no reliable ATMs further up the valley.
  • River evenings are cool, so bring a warm layer even in July.
  • Keep children away from the water's edge; the Kunhar is fast-flowing.
  • In July and August, monsoon rain can slow the Kaghan road, so allow extra travel time.
