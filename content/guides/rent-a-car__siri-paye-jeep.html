Siri Paye jeep from Shogran: price, timing, booking and what the ride is like

Quick answer: The Siri Paye 4×4 jeep with a local driver costs about PKR 12,000 per jeep (about PKR 15,000 in peak season) for the return trip from Shogran with waiting time. One jeep carries up to 6 people. The rough 8-10 km track takes 45-60 minutes each way and climbs to about 9,500 ft. It is usually open from about May to October. Leave by 7 AM for the clearest views. We also run the Kiwai to Shogran climb (about PKR 3,500 per jeep) and Shogran to Manna Meadows (about PKR 10,000 return).

What the ride is like

The track from Shogran to Siri Paye is about 8-10 km of rock and dirt with steep gradients, climbing roughly 1,750 ft to around 9,500 ft. The fact that it takes 45-60 minutes tells you what the surface is like. You will bounce, lean into bends and see sheer drops through the pine trees. Local drivers know every rut and slow down where it matters, and the jeeps use low-range four-wheel drive for the steepest parts.

No car, crossover or ordinary SUV is suitable for this track. Park your car at your Shogran hotel and let the local jeep do the work.

Comfort and safety tips

  • Sit facing forward and hold the grab handles on rough sections.
  • Tell the driver if anyone feels unwell; he can slow down or stop.
  • Bring a warm layer; Siri Paye is much colder than Shogran.
  • Carry cash for tea, horse rides and tips at the meadow; there is no ATM in Shogran.
  • Pregnant travellers and anyone with back problems should check with a doctor before the bumpy ride.

Beyond Siri Paye

The same jeeps run to Manna Meadows, a quieter high pasture near Shogran, for about PKR 10,000 return, subject to conditions. And if the road from Kiwai to Shogran is too steep or wet for your own car, the Kiwai to Shogran jeep for about PKR 3,500 takes you up the climb.

Is the Siri Paye jeep price per person or per jeep?

Per jeep. About PKR 12,000 covers the return trip from Shogran with waiting time, for up to six people.

How long does the jeep take from Shogran to Siri Paye?

About 45-60 minutes each way on a rough 8-10 km track. Plan around three hours in total including time at the meadow.

Can I drive my own car to Siri Paye?

No. The track is too steep and rough for cars and ordinary SUVs. Only local 4×4 jeeps with experienced drivers use it.

Can you take us from Kiwai up to Shogran by jeep?

Yes. The Kiwai to Shogran jeep costs about PKR 3,500 per jeep for the steep climb, useful in wet weather or if you came by bus.

Is the Siri Paye jeep available in winter?

No. Snow closes the Siri Paye track in winter, usually from about November to April.
