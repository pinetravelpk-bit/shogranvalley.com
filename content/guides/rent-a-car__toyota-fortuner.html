Why diesel makes sense here

A diesel engine produces strong pulling power at low revs, which is exactly what a loaded seven-seater needs on the steep 7-9 km climb from Kiwai to Shogran. It also uses noticeably less fuel than petrol V8s over a 500 km round trip from Islamabad, which helps keep the daily rate lower than the Prado or Land Cruiser. With four-wheel drive and high clearance, the Fortuner is ready for rain, rough patches after the monsoon and, when the road is open, winter snow.

Sample winter weekend

  1. Friday: We confirm the Kiwai road status. Pickup in Islamabad, steady drive to Balakot and Kiwai, careful 4×4 climb to a heated room in Shogran.
  2. Saturday: Snow walks in the pine forest, sledging and a bonfire evening. The Siri Paye track is closed in winter.
  3. Sunday: Leave after a late breakfast to descend in daylight and return to Islamabad.

Pair it with our winter snow tour or a room at Pine Park, which lists room heating.

How much is a Fortuner with driver from Islamabad to Shogran?

About PKR 52,000 one way with driver and fuel included. The daily rate is about PKR 32,000, or about PKR 38,000 in peak season.

Is the Fortuner 4×4?

Yes. Our Fortuners have four-wheel drive, high ground clearance, a diesel engine and an automatic gearbox.

Is the Fortuner better than the Prado for Shogran?

For most families it is the better value: similar capability, diesel economy and a lower rate. The Prado offers more luxury.

Can the Fortuner drive in snow to Shogran?

Yes, with an experienced driver, when the road from Kiwai is open. After heavy snowfall the road can close for a day or two.
