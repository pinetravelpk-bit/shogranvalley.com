Shogran's location in numbers

Quick answer: Shogran is at about 34.633° N, 73.467° E, at 7,749 ft (2,362 m), in the Kaghan Valley of Mansehra District, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan. It sits above the village of Kiwai on the Kaghan road (N-15), about 34 km past Balakot, roughly 250 km from Islamabad, and 8-10 km below the Siri Paye meadows.

How to tell a driver where you are going

Ask for "Shogran, via Kiwai on the Kaghan road". Most drivers in Islamabad and Rawalpindi know the route as Hazara Motorway to Mansehra, then Balakot, then Kiwai. If someone suggests a route through Murree and Muzaffarabad, they are thinking of Azad Kashmir, which is a different region.

Where to stay once you know where it is

For Siri Paye mornings, stay on the Shogran plateau at Pine Park Hotel and Resorts, Grand Vista or Sky View. For the river and Sharan Forest, stay further up the valley in Malkandi or Paras.
