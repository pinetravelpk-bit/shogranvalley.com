Why families keep booking the APV

For an extended family or two small families travelling together, the APV removes the hassle of splitting into two cars. Everyone rides together, there is one driver to coordinate, and the cost per person drops sharply. AC keeps the long summer drive through Hazara comfortable, which matters with children and older relatives on board.

The 1500cc engine is the real advantage on this route. On the 7-9 km climb from Kiwai to Shogran, where smaller vans labour with a full load, the APV pulls steadily. On the Kaghan road towards Paras and Naran it keeps a comfortable pace, so long valley days feel shorter.

Seating and luggage

With eight on board, plan for medium bags rather than large hard suitcases, or tell us in advance so the driver can arrange extra space. With six or seven passengers there is room for a full family's luggage on a 3-4 night trip, plus snacks and jackets within reach.

Sample 4-day family trip in the APV

  1. Day 1: Islamabad to Shogran (about 250 km, 5-6 hours), with breakfast near Mansehra and tea at Kawai Waterfall.
  2. Day 2: 7 AM Siri Paye jeep (one or two jeeps for eight people), afternoon walks in Shogran.
  3. Day 3: Drive to Paras for a Sharan Forest jeep trip, overnight by the river in Malkandi.
  4. Day 4: Return to Islamabad.

Things to know

  • The APV is two-wheel drive. From December to February a 4×4 is safer on the snowy Kiwai climb.
  • It does not drive the Siri Paye or Sharan tracks; local jeeps do.
  • Book a week or more ahead for June to August and Eid holidays.
  • Carry cash; there is no ATM in Shogran and the last reliable ATM is in Balakot.
