2 days Shogran and Sharan Forest tour: plan, inclusions and tips

Quick answer: This 2-day, 1-night group tour from Islamabad and Rawalpindi combines Shogran (7,749 ft) with the dense pine forest of Sharan in the Kaghan Valley. It costs about PKR 12,000 per person (minimum 4) and includes Coaster or Hiace transport, 4×4 jeeps from Kiwai to Shogran and to Sharan, one night in a Shogran hotel, breakfast, a local guide and tolls. Day 1 is the drive up and a Shogran evening; Day 2 is Sharan Forest and the drive home.

Day-by-day plan

Day 1: Islamabad to Shogran

  1. Early morning pickup in Islamabad or Rawalpindi, around 5 AM.
  2. Drive via Abbottabad, Mansehra and Balakot along the Kunhar River.
  3. Afternoon arrival at Kiwai and a 4×4 jeep up the steep climb to Shogran.
  4. Check in, rest, then explore Shogran Bazar.
  5. Bonfire evening and overnight in Shogran.

Day 2: Sharan Forest and return

  1. Breakfast at the hotel.
  2. Jeep transfer to Sharan Forest above Paras.
  3. Stream walks, forest trails and photography among tall pine, fir and deodar.
  4. Picnic lunch (own expense or arranged), then back to Shogran or straight to the main road.
  5. Afternoon departure, arriving in Islamabad or Rawalpindi in the evening.

Why combine Shogran with Sharan?

Shogran and Sharan show two sides of the Kaghan Valley. Shogran is an open plateau of pine forest and small hotels with views across the valley, the base for the Siri Paye meadows. Sharan, reached by jeep from Paras, is quieter and denser: old-growth forest, cool streams and very few buildings. Together they make a varied two-day trip without spending all your time in a vehicle.

If you would rather see Siri Paye instead of Sharan, ask us. With one night in Shogran, an early Siri Paye jeep is possible, and many groups prefer it in June and September when the meadow views are clearest.

Packing list

  • Warm jacket and a fleece for Shogran evenings
  • Walking shoes with grip for forest paths and streams
  • A light rain jacket in July and August
  • Cash for lunches, snacks and extras; there is no ATM in Shogran
  • CNIC or passport for hotel check-in and check posts
