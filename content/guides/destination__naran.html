Naran from Shogran: distance, route and what to see

Quick answer: Naran is the main town of the upper Kaghan Valley, about 60 km (2-2½ hours) beyond Shogran on the Kaghan road (N-15). From Shogran you drive down to Kiwai, then up the valley through Paras and Kaghan town to Naran. It is the gateway to Lake Saif-ul-Malook by jeep and, roughly from July to September or October, to Babusar Top about 130 km from Shogran. A day trip is possible, but one night in Naran is far more comfortable.

Shogran and Naran are often treated as rivals, but they work best together. Shogran gives you pine forest, cool air and the Siri Paye meadows; Naran gives you the big lakes and high passes of the upper valley. Most of our travellers spend two nights in Shogran and one or two in Naran.

What to see in and around Naran

Lake Saif-ul-Malook

The best-known lake in the Kaghan Valley lies above Naran, surrounded by snowy peaks. Local jeeps run up from Naran in season. Go early for still water and fewer people. Our Naran Kaghan tour includes the Saif-ul-Malook jeep.

Lulusar Lake and Babusar Top

Beyond Naran the road climbs past Batakundi to Lulusar Lake and over Babusar Top, the high pass towards Chilas and Gilgit-Baltistan. The pass is usually open only from about July to September or October, depending on snow. It is about 130 km from Shogran, so start early.

Naran bazaar and the Kunhar River

Naran town has a busy bazaar, river-side restaurants and plenty of hotels. It is lively in summer and very quiet outside the season.

Suggested Shogran and Naran itineraries

4 days

  1. Islamabad to Shogran, stop at Kawai Waterfall.
  2. Early jeep to Siri Paye, afternoon at leisure in Shogran.
  3. Drive to Naran (2-2½ hours), Lake Saif-ul-Malook in the afternoon or next morning.
  4. Return to Islamabad, about 7-8 hours.

5 days in summer

Add a day for Babusar Top via Batakundi and Lulusar Lake when the pass is open, or a day in Sharan Forest from Paras.

Getting around

An ordinary car is fine for the main road between Shogran, Kiwai and Naran in dry weather. Lake Saif-ul-Malook and Siri Paye need local jeeps. For families, a Honda BR-V or Suzuki APV with driver is comfortable; for groups, a Toyota Hiace. See all options on our rent a car page.

Practical tips

  • Withdraw cash in Balakot. There is no ATM in Shogran, and ATMs further up the valley are not always reliable.
  • Book Naran hotels early for July, August and Eid; the town fills quickly.
  • Carry warm clothes for Saif-ul-Malook and Babusar Top, which are much colder than Naran.
  • Check whether Babusar Top is open before planning to cross it.

Which is better, Shogran or Naran?

They suit different goals. Shogran is quieter and greener, with Siri Paye above it. Naran is the base for Lake Saif-ul-Malook and Babusar Top. With four or five days, visit both.

Is the road from Shogran to Naran open in winter?

The lower Kaghan road is often open, but Naran and the upper valley get heavy snow, and Babusar Top is closed in winter. Most Naran hotels and lake jeeps operate mainly from May to October.

How far is Babusar Top from Shogran?

About 130 km, via Naran, Batakundi and Lulusar Lake. The pass is usually open only from about July to September or October.

Can I do Shogran and Naran in one weekend?

It is possible but rushed. With two nights, choose one; with three or four nights, you can enjoy both without spending most of the trip in the car.
