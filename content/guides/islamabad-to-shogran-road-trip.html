Road trip timeline and stop planner

Quick answer: Leave Islamabad around 6:30 AM, breakfast near Abbottabad or Mansehra (about 8:30), stretch your legs in Balakot (about 10:30, last reliable ATM), have tea at Kawai Waterfall in Kiwai (about 11:30), and reach Shogran after the 7-9 km climb by about 12:30-1 PM. Allow longer on summer weekends and in rain.

Road trip packing for the car

  • Water, snacks and travel-sickness remedies for the winding valley road
  • Jackets within reach; Shogran is far cooler than Islamabad
  • CNIC or passport for police check posts
  • Phone charger and an offline map of the route
  • Cash from Balakot for jeeps, food and tips

Prefer not to drive?

Hand the wheel to a local driver: our cars with driver run from about PKR 16,000 one way to Shogran, and the driver knows every hairpin above Kiwai. Or join a guided tour and let the guide plan every stop.

What time should I arrive in Shogran?

Aim to arrive by early afternoon, so you climb from Kiwai in daylight and have time to settle in before dark.

Where is the best breakfast stop between Islamabad and Shogran?

Abbottabad or Mansehra, about two hours from Islamabad, both have plenty of breakfast options and fuel stations.
