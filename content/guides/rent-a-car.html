Can a normal car reach Shogran?

Yes, in dry weather. The road from Islamabad follows the Hazara Motorway (E-35) towards Mansehra, then Balakot and the Kaghan road along the Kunhar River to Kiwai. From Kiwai a narrow road with tight hairpins climbs 7-9 km to Shogran. A healthy sedan or hatchback with good brakes manages it, but heavy rain, landslides or snow can make it difficult. In winter, from December to February, a 4×4 such as the Fortuner, Prado or Land Cruiser is the safer choice, and on some days the climb closes after heavy snow.

Above Shogran the track to Siri Paye is rough, steep and unpaved for 8-10 km. Only local 4×4 jeeps with experienced drivers use it, so park your car at the hotel and take the Siri Paye jeep for the 45-60 minute ride each way.

Which car is best for a family trip to Shogran?

For 5-7 people the Honda BR-V or Suzuki APV is the most comfortable choice. For a budget family trip, the 7-seat Suzuki Bolan works well. Larger families or two families together usually choose a Toyota Hiace Grand Cabin.

Do I need a 4×4 to visit Shogran in winter?

A 4×4 is strongly recommended from December to February because of snow and ice on the climb from Kiwai. Even then, the road can close after heavy snowfall, so check conditions with our team before you travel.

How much is a jeep from Shogran to Siri Paye?

The Siri Paye 4×4 jeep with a local driver starts at about PKR 12,000 per jeep, not per person, with waiting time at the meadows available. The track is usually open from about May to October.
