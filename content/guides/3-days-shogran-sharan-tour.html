The route day by day

Day 1: Departure, Paras and Sharan Forest

Early departure from Lahore or Islamabad up the Hazara Motorway to Mansehra and Balakot, then along the Kunhar River to Paras. Jeeps carry the group up through dense pine, fir and deodar forest to Sharan. Explore and photograph the forest, then spend the night in or near Sharan as per your package, with dinner included.

Day 2: Sharan to Shogran and Siri Paye

Breakfast with forest views, then a jeep transfer to Shogran. Take in the views from the Shogran plateau at 7,749 ft and continue by jeep to the Siri Paye meadows at about 9,500 ft for photos with snow-capped peaks. Overnight in Shogran or Paras as per package, with dinner included.

Day 3: Return

Breakfast, then a scenic drive back down the valley and home to Lahore or Islamabad, arriving in the evening.

Why three days is the right length from Lahore

Lahore to Shogran is about 600 km, roughly 9-10 hours of driving. A 3-day plan spends the first and last days on the road but still gives a full day in the mountains, with Sharan on the first afternoon and Siri Paye on the second day. Travellers from Islamabad save several hours each way, so they get more time to relax in the forest.

Packing list

  • A warm jacket and fleece; Sharan and Siri Paye are cool even in summer
  • Walking shoes with grip
  • A light rain jacket in July and August
  • Cash for lunches and snacks; there is no ATM in Shogran or Sharan
  • CNIC or passport

What is the difference between the Standard and VIP packages?

The VIP / Deluxe package adds luxury coach transport, a premium hotel or lodge stay in Shogran and a riverside refreshment stop at Paras. Both include the jeeps, meals, guide, photography and drone coverage.

Where do you stay on this tour?

The first night is in or near Sharan Forest and the second in Shogran or Paras, depending on the package.

Can I join from Islamabad instead of Lahore?

Yes. The tour departs from Lahore with Islamabad on the way, so travellers can join in Islamabad.

Are lunches included?

No. The tour includes 3 breakfasts and 2 dinners; lunches and snacks are your own expense.
