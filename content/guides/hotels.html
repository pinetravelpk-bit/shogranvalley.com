Hotels in Shogran, Malkandi and Paras: how to choose the right stay

Book in Shogran if Siri Paye is your priority, and in Malkandi or Paras if you want the river, Sharan Forest and an easier drive towards Naran.

Where to stay: Shogran, Malkandi or Paras?

The Kaghan Valley follows the Kunhar River north from Balakot. Shogran sits on a pine-covered plateau above the village of Kiwai, reached by a steep 7-9 km climb off the main Kaghan road. Malkandi and Paras are further up the valley floor, beside the river and close to the turn for Sharan Forest. Each area suits a different kind of trip.

What to check before you book a hotel in Shogran

  • Heating and hot water. Nights in Shogran are cool even in July and below freezing in winter. All our Shogran hotels list hot water, and Pine Park and Sky View list room heating.
  • Power backup. Electricity cuts are common in the valley, so every hotel here has a generator.
  • Road access in winter. From December to February the climb from Kiwai can be blocked by snow. Ask us about road status before you travel.
  • Cash. There is no ATM in Shogran. The last reliable ATM is in Balakot, so carry enough Pakistani rupees for extras, jeeps and food.
  • Parking and car access. Most visitors drive up from Kiwai in their own car in dry weather. Siri Paye above Shogran needs a 4×4 jeep, which every hotel can help arrange.

For luxury and reliable heating, Pine Park Hotel and Resorts is the most requested hotel in Shogran. Grand Vista is the newest premium option, and Sky View Hotel and Family Resort is the best mid-range choice for families.

How much does a hotel in Shogran cost per night?

Base rates on Shogran Valley start at about PKR 8,000 per night for budget family rooms in Malkandi and Paras, about PKR 13,500 for mid-range rooms in Shogran, and about PKR 20,500 to 28,500 for luxury rooms. Peak season and weekend rates can be higher.

Is it better to stay in Shogran or Naran?

Stay in Shogran for pine forest, cool air and the Siri Paye meadows. Stay in Naran if Lake Saif-ul-Malook or Babusar Top is your main goal. Naran is about 60 km beyond Shogran, so many travellers spend two nights in Shogran and one in Naran.

Do Shogran hotels stay open in winter?

Several hotels stay open in winter for snow trips, but the road from Kiwai can close after heavy snowfall. Confirm road and hotel status with our team before travelling between December and February.
