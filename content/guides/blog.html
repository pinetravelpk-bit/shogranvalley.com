Shogran travel guide hub: start here

Quick answer: These travel guides are written by the Shogran Valley team in Shogran, Kaghan Valley. They answer the questions travellers ask most: where Shogran is (Mansehra District, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, at 7,749 ft), how to get there (about 250 km and 5-6 hours from Islamabad), when to go (May to September for green meadows, December to February for snow), where to stay and what it costs. Use the topics below to find the right guide quickly.

Shogran is a small hill station on a pine-covered plateau above Kiwai on the Kaghan road. It is the base for jeep trips to the Siri Paye meadows, 8-10 km higher at roughly 9,500 ft, and a short drive from Kawai Waterfall, Paras, Sharan Forest and Malkandi. Because conditions change with the seasons, we keep these guides updated with practical, local detail rather than general travel advice.

Is Shogran worth visiting?

Yes, especially if you like pine forests, cool weather and mountain meadows. Shogran is one of the easiest high-altitude escapes from Islamabad, and Siri Paye above it is one of the most photographed meadows in the Kaghan Valley.
