Printable packing checklist by trip type

Quick answer: For any Shogran trip pack layers and a warm jacket, shoes with grip, sun protection, enough cash (no ATM in Shogran; last reliable ATM in Balakot), CNIC or passport, a torch and power bank, and travel-sickness remedies. Add a rain jacket in July and August, and full winter gear from December to February.

Shop before you reach Shogran

Shogran's bazaar is small and seasonal, so buy anything important in Islamabad, Abbottabad, Mansehra or Balakot. Balakot is the last town with reliable ATMs and a good range of shops before the climb from Kiwai.

Can I buy warm clothes in Shogran?

Options are very limited. Bring warm clothes from home or buy them in Islamabad, Mansehra or Balakot on the way.

What should children wear for Siri Paye?

Layers with a warm jacket, a hat, and shoes with grip, even in summer, because the meadow is cool and windy at about 9,500 ft.
