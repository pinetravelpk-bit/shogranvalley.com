Why the Cultus is our most recommended small car

For a couple or a family of three or four, the Cultus is where cost and capability meet. The extra power over a 660cc car is most noticeable on the 7-9 km climb from Kiwai to Shogran, where the road has tight hairpins and steep sections. Its boot also holds bags for four people, not just two, which matters on a mountain trip where everyone needs jackets and warm layers.

On the Hazara Motorway to Mansehra and the main road through Balakot, the Cultus is comfortable and economical. In dry weather it reaches Shogran without trouble. Like any car, it cannot drive the rough track to Siri Paye, so you switch to a local 4×4 jeep for that.

A 3-day Shogran and Naran plan in the Cultus

  1. Day 1: Islamabad to Shogran (about 250 km, 5-6 hours), with a stop at Kawai Waterfall.
  2. Day 2: Early Siri Paye jeep, then drive about 60 km (2-2½ hours) to Naran.
  3. Day 3: Lake Saif-ul-Malook by local jeep in the morning, then drive back to Islamabad.

This route is best from June to September, when the upper valley is fully open.

Travel tips

  • Leave Islamabad early to climb from Kiwai in daylight.
  • Tell us if anyone gets car sick; drivers can plan extra stops on the winding valley road.
  • Bring cash for jeeps and food; there is no ATM in Shogran.

What to confirm when you book

  • Pickup point and time. Most trips start in Islamabad or Rawalpindi between 6 and 8 AM so you reach Kiwai in daylight.
  • Route and stops. Mention Kawai Waterfall, Balakot, Paras or Naran so the driver can plan fuel and breaks.
  • Number of days. A one-way transfer and a multi-day rental are priced differently; we quote whichever is cheaper for your plan.
  • Driver's stay. For overnight trips, your quote confirms where the driver stays and whether that is included.
  • Return date. Tell us early if you might extend, especially in July and August when cars are in demand.

How much does a Suzuki Cultus with driver cost per day?

About PKR 8,000 per day, or about PKR 10,000 in peak season. One way from Islamabad to Shogran with driver and fuel is about PKR 18,000.

Is the Cultus better than the Alto for Shogran?

For four people, yes. The 1000cc engine climbs from Kiwai with less strain and the boot holds more luggage. The Alto is only cheaper for very light travel.

Can the Cultus go to Naran?

Yes, in season. Islamabad to Naran one way costs about PKR 22,000. Lake Saif-ul-Malook and Siri Paye still need local jeeps.

Can you pick us up from Islamabad airport?

Yes. An airport pickup straight to Shogran in the Cultus costs about PKR 19,000.
