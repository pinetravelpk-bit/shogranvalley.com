Important: the Coaster and the climb to Shogran

Our Coaster route prices run to Balakot and Naran along the main Kaghan road. The final 7-9 km road from Kiwai up to Shogran is narrow, with tight hairpins that are difficult for a full-size coach. For Shogran stays, groups usually ride the Coaster to Kiwai and then transfer up to Shogran in smaller vehicles or local jeeps, which we can help arrange as part of the plan. Tell us your hotel and we will confirm the best way to get the whole group and its luggage up.

Why groups choose a Coaster

  • Everyone together. One vehicle keeps the group, the teachers or the organisers in one place.
  • Proper coach comfort. Reclining seats, AC throughout and room to move along the aisle.
  • Storage. Overhead racks keep day bags and jackets within reach; main luggage goes in the luggage area.
  • PA system. Guides and organisers can brief the group on the way.
  • Lower cost per head than several vans.

Sample 3-day school or corporate tour

  1. Day 1: Islamabad to Balakot and the Kaghan road, Kawai Waterfall stop, overnight in the valley.
  2. Day 2: Naran and Lake Saif-ul-Malook by local jeeps, group dinner.
  3. Day 3: Return to Islamabad with a lunch stop in Balakot.

How many people fit in a Toyota Coaster?

Twenty-two passengers in reclining seats, plus the driver.

Can the Coaster go up to Shogran?

The narrow hairpin road from Kiwai to Shogran is not well suited to a full-size coach. Groups usually transfer to smaller vehicles or jeeps at Kiwai, which we can help arrange.
