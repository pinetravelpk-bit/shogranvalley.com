Itinerary

Day 1: Islamabad to Shogran

Leave Islamabad around 5 AM in an AC vehicle and drive via Abbottabad, Mansehra and Balakot, with a breakfast stop in Balakot. At Kiwai the family switches to a 4×4 jeep for the 7-9 km climb to Shogran, arriving around lunchtime. Afternoon rest and a stroll in Shogran Bazar, then a bonfire and family dinner at the hotel.

Day 2: Siri Paye and Sharan Forest

After breakfast, take the jeep up to the Siri Paye meadows at about 9,500 ft: horse rides for the children, photos with Makra Peak behind, and a picnic on the grass. After lunch in Shogran, head to Sharan Forest for a walk among tall pines and streams, returning to the hotel in the evening.

Day 3: Kawai Waterfall, Kaghan and home

Check out after breakfast and take the jeep down to Kiwai. Stop at Kawai Waterfall for photos, then visit the local market in Kaghan town. After lunch, begin the drive back to Islamabad, arriving in the evening.

Best season

May to September is ideal. June and September usually bring the clearest Siri Paye mornings; July and August are the greenest but busiest and wettest, so book two to three weeks ahead during school holidays. The Siri Paye track is closed by snow in winter.
